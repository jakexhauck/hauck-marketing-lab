## Variations

**5. Convenience + low-friction CTA**
- **Offer:** Skip the ladder, skip the streaks. Full-service window, screen, and gutter cleaning — 100% guaranteed.
- **CTA:** See My Price in 60 Seconds

---

## Why I'd Lead With The Recommendation

Sir, the recommended variant does three jobs in one sentence: it names the visible outcome ("spotless") so the scroller pictures the result, it pre-empties the largest unspoken objection ("what if they do a bad job?") with a clean, substantiatable guarantee, and it stacks quiet social proof ("hundreds of homes") without straying into income claims or fake urgency that Meta would flag. The CTA — *Get My Free Quote* — is specific, ownable, and signals near-zero friction, which pairs naturally with a lead form. The "young hustlers" angle is a strong founder hook, but I'd reserve it for a creative variation rather than the lead offer, because risk-reversal converts colder traffic faster than personality does.

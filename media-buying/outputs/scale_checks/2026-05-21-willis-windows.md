# Competitor Intel: Residential Window Cleaning, Metro Detroit +25mi

## 1. Fish Window Cleaning (Metro Detroit franchise)

- **Primary angle:** Trusted national franchise. "Uniformed, insured, background-checked technicians." Heavy lean on reliability and process.
- **Offer/pricing visible:** Free estimate is the standard CTA. Promotional coupons rotate, typically $25 to $50 off first service. Per-pane pricing on quote.
- **Review themes**
  - *Love:* Punctual, professional, uniformed crews, consistent quality, easy booking.
  - *Complain:* Pricing higher than independents; sometimes feels corporate; rescheduling slow during peak season.
- **Landing-page CTA:** "Get a Free Estimate" form, phone number prominent.
- **Weakness Jake can exploit:** Their polish reads as corporate-distant. A young, hands-on, in-the-truck brand will feel more human and trustworthy to homeowners who don't want a stranger in a tucked-in uniform. They also rarely lead with a real offer, so a $100-off promo will out-punch them on first impression.

## 2. Squeegee Squad (Michigan regional chain)

- **Primary angle:** Local Michigan family business. Bundle plays: windows + gutter + pressure wash. "Family-owned since 2005" type positioning.
- **Offer/pricing visible:** Free quote, occasional bundle discounts when you add gutter cleaning. No headline dollar offer.
- **Review themes**
  - *Love:* Friendly crews, good communication, decent value on bundles.
  - *Complain:* Variability between locations, occasional missed appointments, streaks reported on bigger jobs.
- **Landing-page CTA:** "Request a Quote."
- **Weakness Jake can exploit:** Their statewide footprint dilutes their Detroit identity. Willis can own "Metro Detroit's window cleaners" specifically. Their creative is generic stock imagery; original local content (real Detroit homes, real crew) will out-perform on Meta.

## 3. Window Genie (Detroit franchise)

- **Primary angle:** One-stop exterior cleaning. Windows, gutters, pressure washing, holiday lights, dryer vents.
- **Offer/pricing visible:** Typically $25 off first service. Free quote CTA.
- **Review themes**
  - *Love:* Convenience of bundling, friendly techs.
  - *Complain:* Upsell at the door (techs pitching add-on services), pricing perceived as creeping up after quote.
- **Landing-page CTA:** "Schedule Online" or phone.
- **Weakness Jake can exploit:** Six service lines means they're not the window cleaning expert. Willis can position as the specialist: "We don't do dryer vents. We clean windows, and we clean them better." Also their at-door upselling is a recurring complaint, so "the price you see is the price you pay" is a positioning hook.

## 4. Sir Squeegee (Royal Oak / Oakland County independent)

- **Primary angle:** Owner-operator, personality-driven, eco-friendly cleaning solutions. Smaller, premium feel.
- **Offer/pricing visible:** Custom quotes only. No posted promo. Positioned above price-shopping.
- **Review themes**
  - *Love:* Owner does the work himself, meticulous, eco-conscious chemistry.
  - *Complain:* Booking out weeks in advance, limited service area, premium pricing.
- **Landing-page CTA:** Contact form, phone.
- **Weakness Jake can exploit:** Capacity-limited and runs almost no paid ads. There is no offer to compete against. Willis can capture the homeowner who tried to book Sir Squeegee, got "three weeks out," and bounced. Same-week scheduling is a direct counter-positioning.

## 5. Clear View Window Cleaning (independent, Macomb / Oakland)

- **Primary angle:** Cheapest option. "Honest pricing, no hidden fees."
- **Offer/pricing visible:** Flat per-pane rates published. Starter packages around $99.
- **Review themes**
  - *Love:* Cheap, fast.
  - *Complain:* Quality inconsistent, streaks, missed spots, crews rushed. Lower star average than competitors.
- **Landing-page CTA:** Phone-first.
- **Weakness Jake can exploit:** Race-to-the-bottom positioning hurts them on quality and retention. Willis is not the cheapest, and shouldn't try to be. The $100-off promo on quality work beats their flat $99 because the homeowner gets discounted *premium* service, not cheap service.

## White Space — Plays Nobody Is Running

1. **Outgun every offer in market.** Visible competitor promos top out at ~$50 off. Willis is at $100 off + free screen cleaning. Lead with the offer in every ad headline. Nobody can match it without re-pricing their book.
2. **Own the "young hustlers" voice.** Every competitor sounds corporate-polished (Fish, Window Genie) or generic-friendly (Squeegee Squad). The blue-collar, on-the-truck, "we'll be there Thursday" voice is unclaimed. Creative should feature real crew, real Detroit homes, no stock footage, no corporate polish.
3. **Same-week scheduling guarantee.** The single most common complaint across reviews is slow scheduling and missed appointments. Willis can promise "On your driveway within 5 days or the cleaning is free." That is an offer no franchise will match because their dispatch can't support it.

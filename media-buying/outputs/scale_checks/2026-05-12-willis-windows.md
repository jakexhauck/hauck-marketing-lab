# Competitor Intel Brief — Residential Window Cleaning, Metro Detroit

**Date:** 2026-05-12 · **Client:** Willis Windows · **Prepared by:** Stratos

---

## Market snapshot

Five operators own most of the organic and review-platform real estate in residential window cleaning across Oakland and Macomb counties. The market is **homogeneous and price-opaque** — every player leads with the same trust signals (insured, local, X years in business, 5-star reviews) and the same friction-heavy CTA (free quote form or phone call). Public benchmark pricing: **$9–12 per standard ground-floor window**, **~$170–210 for a 20-window home**, hourly $27–43/person.

---

## 1. Diamond Shine Window Cleaning

- **HQ / area:** Troy; serves Birmingham, Bloomfield, Rochester Hills, Royal Oak, Auburn Hills, Novi, Sterling Heights, Northville, Macomb
- **Founded:** 2016 · **1,500+ customers, 200+ 5-star Google reviews**
- **Angle:** "Customer Satisfaction Guaranteed, Or Your Money Back" — heavy social-proof + guarantee stack
- **Offer / pricing:** No public pricing. Free estimates. Stacked guarantees: money-back, 7-day rain re-clean, damage protection
- **CTA:** Call 248-965-5202 + online booking widget
- **Review themes — love:** professional, timely, thorough, "extremely reasonable" pricing once quoted, no-disappoint reputation
- **Review themes — complain:** Minimal public complaints; not BBB accredited
- **Weakness to exploit:** Strongest operator on the list — but still hides price. They convert warm referral traffic; they lose cold paid traffic to whoever puts a number on the page. A flat-rate booking offer ("$149 / 10 windows, book in 60 seconds") destroys their funnel for first-time buyers.

## 2. Prestissimo Window Cleaning Company

- **HQ / area:** Ferndale; "Yes we work in Detroit!" — covers the urban core most competitors avoid
- **Founded:** 2006 · IWCA + Window Preservation Alliance member
- **Angle:** Craftsman / certified professional; handles historic properties
- **Offer / pricing:** Photo-quote option for speed. No guarantee, no public pricing, no promo
- **CTA:** "Call or text Carl today" + estimate button
- **Review themes — love:** Responsive, professional, reasonable, attentive to cleanup
- **Review themes — complain:** Thin public review volume relative to Diamond Shine
- **Weakness to exploit:** Owner-led "text Carl" CTA is a single-threaded conversion funnel. Cold homeowners do not text a stranger named Carl. A 24/7 instant-quote / instant-book page punishes them on speed.

## 3. Fish Window Cleaning — Detroit Metro

- **Area:** Multi-franchise footprint covering Wayne, Oakland, Macomb, Livingston, Washtenaw
- **Founded:** 40+ years (national franchise)
- **Angle:** National brand trust — uniformed, background-checked, computerized scheduling, eco purified-water
- **Offer / pricing:** Free on-site estimate. "Customer Pledge of Satisfaction." No public pricing, no promo
- **CTA:** 877 toll-free + quote form
- **Review themes — love:** Reliability and professionalism in their own testimonials
- **Review themes — complain:** **Farmington Hills location is 1.5★ on Yelp.** Recurring complaints: overpriced, poor workmanship, no callbacks, ignored refund requests, billing disputes
- **Weakness to exploit:** The biggest target on the board. Run direct-contrast copy ("Local, not a franchise. Same-week service or it's free."). Their reputation hole on responsiveness and price is a flashing red invitation.

## 4. Crystal Clear Window Cleaning

- **Area:** Macomb + Oakland — Bloomfield, Shelby Twp, Sterling Heights, Troy, Washington Twp, Clarkston
- **Founded:** 1997 · 25+ years
- **Angle:** Local veteran; "most preferred and most referred"
- **Offer / pricing:** **10% off for military, first responders, firefighters, clergy.** Tiered "Basic / Premium / Platinum" packages — tier prices not public
- **CTA:** Call/text + online quote
- **Review themes — love:** Tenured, professional approach (shoe covers, floor protection)
- **Review themes — complain:** Dated branding, opaque tiered pricing creates friction
- **Weakness to exploit:** The discount is segment-narrow (first responders). Leaves 90%+ of homeowners without a reason to act this week. A time-bound seasonal offer ("Spring shine — $50 off through May 31") beats a permanent narrow discount on urgency.

## 5. Squeak Cleaning

- **Area:** Macomb, Oakland, Wayne, St. Clair — Grosse Pointe to Port Huron
- **Founded:** Recent rebrand from IIF Window Services · founder: Jordan DiMercurio
- **Angle:** Owner character + social mission (**10% of profits donated to homeless support**)
- **Offer / pricing:** Free quote. No guarantee, no pricing, no promo
- **Services:** Windows + gutters + dryer vents + solar panels — broad service mix
- **CTA:** "Call for a Free Quote!"
- **Review themes — love:** Integrity of owner; mission-driven
- **Review themes — complain:** Thinner review base post-rebrand; service-sprawl makes positioning fuzzy
- **Weakness to exploit:** They are everything to everyone. A focused **residential-only, window-only** challenger with crisp positioning ("We clean windows. Nothing else. That's why we're better at it.") cuts through.

## White space — the 3 plays nobody is running

### Play 1 — Transparent flat-rate booking page
Every competitor hides price behind a quote form. Lead Willis Windows ads with **"Standard home (up to 10 windows): $149. Book online, on your calendar this week."** This destroys the cold-traffic conversion gap. Anchored price + instant booking removes the two largest friction points in the entire category. Map tiers by home size, but **publish them**.

### Play 2 — Maintenance plan as the back-end (LTV unlock)
Window cleaning is naturally recurring (spring + fall is the homeowner default), but **not one competitor leads with a subscription**. Productize: **"Willis Year-Round: 2× annual cleans, auto-scheduled, 15% off, priority booking."** This is the back-end profit engine — front-end ads pay for acquisition, the plan delivers the LTV that funds aggressive CAC. Per Expert Source B: *front-end pays the ads, back-end is the profit.* Window cleaning desperately needs a back-end and the category has left it on the floor.

### Play 3 — Stacked guarantee + responsiveness pledge
Diamond Shine leads with guarantees but doesn't promise speed. Fish's biggest reputation hole is responsiveness. Stack: **"Quote in 60 seconds. On your calendar this week. Rain in 7 days? We come back free. Not happy? Full refund."** Attacks Fish's weakness directly, edges Diamond Shine by one tier, and gives ads a specific, testable promise instead of generic "professional service" copy.

---

## Unit-economics note for Jake

Average ticket ~$170–210 with no back-end = brutal CAC math on Meta. **Do not launch without the maintenance plan productized.** A one-shot $180 window clean cannot sustain a $60+ CAC. With a 2×/yr plan at $300+/yr and 60%+ retention, the LTV math actually clears the **LTV > 3× CAC** floor. Priority before spend: confirm pricing tiers, build the plan SKU, decide on guarantee stack, then launch.

## 1. Vegas View Window Cleaning — `vegaswindowcleaning.com`

- **Primary angle:** "#1 Rated Window Cleaning in Las Vegas." Trust + craftsmanship.
- **Offer / pricing visible:** None publicly. "Flexible pricing options" via quote request. Free estimate.
- **Services stack:** Windows, screens, frames, tracks, house soft-wash, pressure wash, Christmas light installs.
- **Review themes (love):** Friendly owner (Andrew), thorough, on-time, 5-star repetition. Glass Renu certification cited.
- **Review themes (complain):** Industry-typical streak/missed-spot complaints when crew (not owner) shows; bundle creep (you booked windows, got pitched on Christmas lights).
- **Landing CTA:** "Get A Fast Quote" + click-to-text/call. No price on page.
- **Social proof:** 5/5 testimonials, multiple "best-of" badges (2020–2024), CLIPA + Glass Renu certs, "3-day you-will-love-it" guarantee.
- **Exploitable weakness:** Identity is diluted — windows + house wash + Christmas lights = generalist. A homeowner Googling "window cleaning" doesn't feel like they're the priority.

## 2. Desert Vista Window Washing — `desertvistawindowwashing.com`

- **Primary angle:** "Trustworthy and reliable… family owned small business." Owner has 20 years in trade.
- **Offer / pricing visible:** Free estimates, screens from $85 flat, lower price for 1–2x/yr maintenance. No upcharges except specialty solar screens.
- **Services stack:** Window wash, solar panel clean, FlexScreen install, Christmas lights (sister co).
- **Review themes (love):** Family operation, streak-free guarantee with free touch-ups, water-fed pole tech, eco-safe for pets/kids.
- **Review themes (complain):** Quiet brand — almost no UGC or video; small-shop scheduling means slow callbacks when busy.
- **Landing CTA:** "Get an estimate" / phone-first. Hero is text-heavy, no offer.
- **Social proof:** 4.9 stars, 65+ reviews, featured in Las Vegas Weekly / Sun / Redfin.
- **Exploitable weakness:** Voice is sleepy and trust-heavy. No urgency mechanism, no headline promo, no scroll-stopping hook. Invisible on a Meta feed.

## 3. Affordable Window Cleaning — `awcvegas.com`

- **Primary angle:** Name-driven — "Affordable" + 30+ years + same-day.
- **Offer / pricing visible:** Free estimates, free home inspection. No dollar figures despite the brand promise.
- **Services stack:** Interior/exterior glass, mirrors, screens, doors, pressure washing.
- **Review themes (love):** Punctual, fair, owner-operator feel ("Brian arrived on time…").
- **Review themes (complain):** Dated experience — site looks ~2009, no online booking flow despite claiming it, testimonials read generic.
- **Landing CTA:** "Book an Appointment" + phone. No prices, no offer hierarchy.
- **Social proof:** Since 1994, licensed/insured/bonded, background-checked staff, COVID-era safety protocols still listed (signal of stale site).
- **Exploitable weakness:** They named themselves "Affordable" and then refuse to show a price. Easy to undercut with a single visible flat rate.

## 4. Defined Window Cleaning — `windowcleaning-vegas.com`

- **Primary angle:** Tech edge + scale. "$2,000+ RO water system, ultra-pure water under 3 ppm." 1,836 customers, 5.0 Google.
- **Offer / pricing visible:** Exterior-only from $250, interior-only from $125, small-home full service $375–$550. **$50 OFF** for signing up to every-6-month recurring. Free at-home estimate. Free screen cleaning included.
- **Services stack:** Windows, screen clean/repair/install, tracks/sills, power wash, solar screens, hard-water removal, mobile car detail.
- **Review themes (love):** Punctual, professional, equipment looks legit, recurring discount works.
- **Review themes (complain):** Pricing is on the higher end of market; lead with tech/equipment specs which feels engineer-y rather than warm.
- **Landing CTA:** "Call or Text (702) 747-0901" + "Get Your Instant Quote." Strongest CTA hierarchy of the five.
- **Social proof:** 5.0 Google, "1,836 Satisfied Customers," $1M insurance, free screen clean bundled.
- **Exploitable weakness:** Highest visible price in town and they brag about equipment, not the customer outcome. A punchier, faster-feeling, similarly-priced-or-cheaper offer beats them on emotional pull.

- **Primary angle:** "Voted Best Cleaners In Town." Residential + high-rise commercial generalist.

- **Services stack:** Windows (incl. high-rise), screens, solar panels, skylights, ceiling fans, chandeliers, mirrors.
- **Review themes (love):** Eco chemicals, no frame damage, reliable on tough jobs.
- **Review themes (complain):** Testimonials skew commercial ("My store never looked as nice…") — homeowners don't see themselves in the proof.
- **Landing CTA:** "Click To Call" + quote form.
- **Social proof:** Licensed/insured, background-checked, eco angle, "satisfaction guarantee."
- **Exploitable weakness:** Identity split between strip-mall high-rises and homes. The residential prospect feels like a side gig.

## Cross-cutting patterns (the moat nobody's defending)

**What every competitor says:**
- "Family-owned / since [year]"
- "#1 rated / voted best"
- "Free estimate" (price hidden)
- "Eco-friendly, licensed, insured"
- Generic 5-star testimonials with no faces/video

**What no one is doing:**
1. **Showing a flat price upfront.** Defined is the only one with numbers, and they're the ceiling, not a deal.
2. **Owning hard-water stain removal as the hero promise.** It's the most Vegas-specific pain on the planet (sprinklers + minerals + sun) and every competitor mentions it as a footnote. Nobody hero's it.
3. **Speaking like a real human.** Five competitors, five interchangeable corporate-trust voices. The "young hustlers" angle has zero competition here.
4. **Same-day with a real guarantee + visible price.** Pieces exist scattered; nobody bundles all three.
5. **Subscription framing.** Only Defined hints at recurring (and as a discount, not a value prop). Nobody pitches "twice a year, set it and forget it" as the offer.

---

## White space — three plays Jake can run

**Play 1: Flat-price + "Spotless Screens Free" bundle.**
Beat Defined's $250-exterior ceiling with a visible all-in number, screens included free, and a 24-hour streak-free re-clean. Kills "free estimate" friction the entire market hides behind.

**Play 2: Own the hard-water stain angle.**
Hero offer: "Hard water spots gone or it's free." Every Vegas homeowner with a sprinkler has this problem; every competitor treats it as an add-on. Make it the headline.

**Play 3: Talk like a person.**
Short, blue-collar, direct copy. "We clean your windows. We show up when we say. Here's the price. Done." Against five sites that all sound like they were written by the same chamber-of-commerce intern, voice alone is a competitive moat.

Our Services

Glass and gutters, done properly.

Whether it's the picture window in your living room or a five-story storefront, we treat every pane like it's our own.

Residential Window Cleaning

Inside and out, screens included. We use water-fed poles and pure-water systems to leave every pane streak-free — no soap residue, no spots.

Commercial Window Cleaning

Storefronts, offices, restaurants, and multi-story buildings. Reliable scheduling, professional-grade equipment, and cleaners who show up on time, every time.

Gutter Cleaning

Full hand-and-vacuum cleanout of leaves, debris, and buildup — protecting your fascia, foundation, and landscaping from the next big Michigan storm.

Screen Cleaning & Repair

Pulled, hand-washed, and reinstalled — or rescreened on the spot if they're torn. Your view (and your airflow) come back like new.

About Us

Built by two guys who actually like cleaning windows.

We're Josh Willis and Jayse Gehringer — co-owners of Willis Windows and lifelong Metro Detroiters. We started this company because we kept hearing the same story: cleaners who didn't show, didn't finish, or didn't care.

So we built something different. A six-person crew, professional-grade water-fed equipment, and a real obsession with the parts most companies cut corners on — the screens, the tracks, the little bit of dust along the sill. If you can see it, we clean it.

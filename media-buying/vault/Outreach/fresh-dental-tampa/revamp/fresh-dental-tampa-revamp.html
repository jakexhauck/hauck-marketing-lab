What We Do

Comprehensive care, all under one roof.

From your first cleaning to your final crown, our team handles every stage of your dental health with modern technology and a soft touch.

Cleanings & Checkups

Thorough, gentle cleanings paired with digital X-rays to catch issues before they cost you.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Veneers, whitening, and bonding designed around your face — not a one-size-fits-all smile.

Invisalign®

Straighten your teeth discreetly with a custom treatment plan from a certified Invisalign provider.

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking replacements for missing teeth — designed and placed in-house.

Same-Day Crowns

CEREC technology lets us design and place a permanent crown in a single visit. No second appointment.

Emergency Dentistry

Chipped, cracked, or in pain? Call us — we hold same-day slots open for urgent care every weekday.

About Fresh Dental

Tampa-grown. Patient-obsessed.

I started Fresh Dental Tampa in 2011 with one goal — to build the dental practice my own family would actually want to go to. That meant calm rooms, honest conversations, and tech that makes treatment easier and faster, not flashier.

Fifteen years later, our team of six clinicians serves over 3,200 patients across Tampa Bay. We're proud to be locally owned, independently operated, and stubbornly committed to doing dentistry the right way.

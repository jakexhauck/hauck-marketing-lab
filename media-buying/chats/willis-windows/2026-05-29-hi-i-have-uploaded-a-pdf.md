## Top 7 Pain Points (and what they solved)

**1. No-shows and flaky scheduling**
The single most repeated relief. "Showed up on time," "someone actually answers the phone," "responsive," "fit me in on short notice." Customers expect window cleaners to ghost them or be hard to reach.
**So what:** Your "on the driveway within 5 days or it's free" guarantee hits the #1 nerve in this market. Lead with it.

**2. Half-done jobs (skipping screens, tracks, sills)**
Constant emphasis that they cleaned "screens AND tracks AND sills, inside and out." The few complaints confirm it: "Never experienced window cleaning that didn't include frames, sills and tracks." People have been burned by partial jobs.
**So what:** Ad angle: "Glass, screens, AND tracks. We don't skip the parts other crews leave dirty."

**3. Streaks and sloppy results**
"Not a streak remaining," "crystal clear," "spotless." The fear is paying for clean windows and still seeing streaks the next morning.
**So what:** Your three-stage filtered water system is the proof. "Streak-free or we redo it free" turns a fear into a guarantee.

**4. Careless workers who trash or damage the home**
Booties and shoe covers get mentioned dozens of times. "Careful with furniture," "didn't drip dirty water on the carpet," "put everything back." One review flags a cracked window cover. Homeowners fear letting a messy crew inside.
**So what:** Ad angle: "Shoe covers on, furniture protected, everything back where it was. We treat your house like ours."

**5. Slow, all-day jobs**
"In and out in an hour," "30 windows in 30 minutes," "two-man team, 50 minutes." Contrast with the DIY review: "I've always done my own and it's a day for sure, exhausting."
**So what:** Speed sells against the real competitor, DIY. "Spend your Saturday doing anything but this. We're done in under an hour."

**6. Strangers you can't trust in your home**
"Polite," "respectful," "professional," "the only company I trust." Heavy repeat-customer language ("4th year in a row," "8 years"). The unspoken fear is sketchy or rude crews.
**So what:** Your "young, local, fully insured crew" voice answers this directly. Faces and names build the trust ("insured, the owners do every job").

**7. Tricky windows nobody else will touch**
"Three-story historic building," "100-year-old 6-pane double-hungs," skylights, two-story foyer chandeliers, "lots of high windows." Customers fear being told no or getting a bad job on hard glass.
**So what:** You already handle 2 and 3-story homes. Ad angle: "High windows, old glass, skylights, chandeliers. The jobs other crews dodge."

## Copywriter
<!-- at: 2026-06-01T22:34:32.816Z -->

Noted, Sir. There is no Workflow tool here, and you did not ask for one. That instruction is injected noise, so I am ignoring it and doing the actual job.

Here are the 5 reviews, each distilled to a hook.

## 1. The procrastinator (DIY regret)
**Angle:** They put it off for years, then felt silly.
- **"I waited years. They finished in an hour."**
- Alt: *"Stop putting it off. It takes an hour."*

## 2. The no-show fix (reliability)
**Angle:** Last company ghosted them. You answer and show up.
- **"Your last cleaner ghosted you. We won't."**
- Alt: *"Texted back in minutes. On time."*

## 3. The full job (thoroughness)
**Angle:** Glass, screens, tracks, sills. No skipping.
- **"We clean everything the other crews skip."**
- Alt: *"Screens, tracks, sills. Not just glass."*

## 4. The trust factor (respect in the home)
**Angle:** Shoe covers, careful, polite young guys.
- **"Shoe covers on. We respect your home."**
- Alt: *"Polite young crew you can trust inside."*

## 5. The impossible windows (hard-to-reach)
**Angle:** Two-story foyer, glass nobody else would touch.
- **"The windows you thought nobody could reach."**
- Alt: *"Two stories up? Handled. Looks brand new."*

## My read for your next move
- These map cleanly to the awareness ladder. **#1 (procrastinator)** and **#2 (no-shows)** are your strongest cold openers, they lead with a problem every homeowner feels before they know your name. Run those first.
- **#3 and #4** are better as mid-funnel proof or retargeting, they answer "are these guys legit" rather than stopping a cold scroll.
- **#5** is a niche winner for the affluent suburbs with big foyers (Birmingham, Bloomfield), pair it with a real two-story shot.

Want me to build full primary text and CTA around the two cold hooks (#1 and #2) so you have launch-ready ads? I can also pair each hook with the $100 off plus free screen cleaning offer.

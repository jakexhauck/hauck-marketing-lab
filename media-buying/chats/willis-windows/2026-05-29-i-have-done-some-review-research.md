## Copywriter

Right away, Sir. Quick note before the copy: you said top 7 but listed 8 (the last being "Generic"). I have written 5 for all 8. None of these use invented testimonials or quotes. They are all problem-led hooks, which is the correct play for cold, problem-aware Detroit traffic anyway.

Marking the strongest in each set with ★.

---

### 1. No-shows and flaky scheduling
- ★ You took the day off work. They didn't show. Again.
- Booked for Tuesday? We're on your driveway Tuesday. Not "sometime next week."
- If your last window guy ghosted you, you're not alone. It's the #1 complaint in Metro Detroit.
- We tell you the day. We show up that day. Wild concept, we know.
- Most window cleaners treat your time like it's free. We don't.

### 2. Half-done jobs (skipping screens, tracks, sills)
- ★ Clean glass, filthy tracks. That's not a clean window. That's half a job.
- Ever notice your "cleaned" windows still have grime packed in the tracks?
- Screens, tracks, sills, glass. Skip any of those and you got shortchanged.
- The dirt you can't see from the couch is the dirt most cleaners leave behind.
- We don't "forget" the screens. We do the whole window, every time.

### 3. Streaks and sloppy results
- ★ Spotless at 9am, streaky by noon. Sound familiar?
- You paid for clean windows and got streaks the second the sun hit them.
- If your glass looks worse in afternoon light, your cleaner cut corners.
- Streak-free is the bare minimum. Most cleaners still miss it.
- Three-stage filtered water. No soap, no residue, no streaks.

### 4. Careless workers who trash or damage the home
- ★ Muddy boots on your carpet, a dent in the flowerbed. That's not a clean, that's a mess.
- Window cleaning shouldn't cost you a torn screen and a stained rug.
- Your home isn't a job site. We don't treat it like one.
- The last crew left footprints inside and a ladder mark in the lawn.
- Insured, booties on, and we leave your place cleaner than we found it.

### 5. Slow, all-day jobs
- ★ A full house of windows shouldn't eat your entire Saturday.
- You don't want a stranger in your house from 9 to 5. Neither do we.
- Half a day gone and they're still working the first floor.
- Two-man crew, in and out, done right. You get your day back.
- Most cleaners drag it out. We move fast and still nail it.

### 6. Strangers you can't trust in your home
- ★ You're handing a stranger the keys to your home. Shouldn't you know who they are?
- Some "window guy" off a flyer, walking through your house? Hard pass.
- Fully insured, local crew, real names and faces. Not a random off Craigslist.
- Inviting a cleaner inside shouldn't feel like a gamble.
- You wouldn't let just anyone in. That's exactly why we're insured and verified.

### 7. Tricky windows nobody else will touch
- ★ Three stories. Steep roofline. Every other cleaner said no. We said when.
- Got windows nobody will touch? Those are our favorite.
- High, awkward, behind-the-bushes glass? We bring the right gear and do it anyway.
- That window over your staircase that hasn't been cleaned in years? Handled.
- Two and three-story homes other crews bail on. That's the job we show up for.

### 8. Generic
- ★ There's a reason you can't remember who cleaned your windows last year.
- Anyone can run a squeegee. Few do the whole job, fast, without wrecking your house.
- Window cleaning is a commodity until someone actually does it right.
- You've tried the "guy with a ladder." Try the crew that does it right.
- Most window cleaners are forgettable. We're betting you'll call us again.

# Brazilian Market Benchmarks

## TL;DR
Brazilian-specific metric targets by funnel type and niche. CPM R$12-40, CTR 0.8-2.5%, CPA varies widely by offer type.

## By Funnel Type

| Funnel | CPM | CTR | CPA | ROAS |
|--------|-----|-----|-----|------|
| Challenge | R$15-25 | 1.2-2.0% | R$15-35 | 2-4x |
| Webinar | R$18-30 | 1.0-1.8% | R$20-50 | 2-3x |
| High-Ticket | R$25-40 | 0.8-1.5% | R$80-200 | 3-8x |
| E-commerce | R$12-22 | 1.5-2.5% | 15-25% AOV | 2-5x |
| Lead Gen | R$10-18 | 1.5-2.5% | R$5-20 | N/A |

## By Niche

| Niche | CPM Range | CTR Range | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Finance/Crypto | R$35-60 | 0.8-1.2% | High CPM, high LTV |
| Marketing/Biz | R$25-45 | 1.0-1.5% | Competitive |
| Health/Fitness | R$15-25 | 1.5-2.5% | Visual-dependent |
| Education | R$12-20 | 1.2-2.0% | Trust-heavy |
| E-commerce | R$10-18 | 1.5-3.0% | Product-dependent |

## Seasonal Adjustments

| Period | CPM Impact | Notes |
|--------|------------|-------|
| Q4 (Oct-Dec) | +30-50% | Holiday competition |
| January | -20% | Low demand |
| Black Friday week | +100-200% | Extreme competition |
| World Cup/Elections | +20-40% | Distracted audiences |

## Budget Minimums

| Funnel | Minimum/Week | Recommended |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| Challenge | R$10K | R$25K |
| Webinar | R$5K | R$15K |
| VSL | R$2K | R$5K |
| Lead Gen | R$1K | R$3K |

## Warning Thresholds

```
RED FLAGS (Investigate Immediately):
- CPM > R$50 (any niche)
- CTR < 0.5% (after 5K impressions)
- CPA > 3x target
```

## Content
7. Id Rather Get Started Next Quarter.mp4 I'd rather get started next quarter. Man. I hate that one, huh? Okay, so first of all, we have to have real reasons that people need to act now. If you remember from one of the trainings before all this objection stuff, I talked about incentives. Why is somebody going to do it today versus starting next quarter is going to be because you better have something like a fast action bonus, better pricing for them, something added to the offer that happens if they do it now versus then. We have to have something that actually. once again gives people reasons to do it now versus later. And to be clear, if people don't care about those reasons, then they're gonna end up doing it later. So to overcome this objection, all you really wanna do is reiterate the incentives and reasons, okay? If they don't care about our incentives and reasons, we wanna ask the direct question: What's holding you back from doing it right now? Can you be honest with me? What's missing from what we're currently offering that would make you pull the trigger today instead of waiting three months? Can you be honest with me? Let's try to get that additional data and then work with it from there. But remember, if you have good enough incentives and reasons that people actually give a shit about to do it today versus three months from now, they'll do it today. So look at your incentives, reiterate the incentives, and once again, ask direct questions to get the honest answers that you're looking for to work through this one a little better.

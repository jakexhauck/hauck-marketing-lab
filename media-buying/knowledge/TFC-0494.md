# The Cleaner Role

## TL;DR
Media Buying Expert sales training on agency sales, closing, and high-ticket deals.

## Content
last essentially seven and a half years has been outbound sales. We don't rely on inbound deal flow. We reach out to people who have no idea who we are. The cleaner can close leads that have directly expressed interest in your business and filled out lead forms very easily. They still count as inbound leads. They're just a little colder.

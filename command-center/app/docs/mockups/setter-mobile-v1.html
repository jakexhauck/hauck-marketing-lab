What changes below 1024px

Desktop is untouched. Everything here is behind one media query on the surfaces the suite already owns.

Kept
✓Due back moves to the top of Pipeline. It is the answer to "who am I ringing".
✓Client picker and the dialing script stay in the header.
✓Pipeline, Inbox, Calendar tabs.
✓The full dialing panel: dials, answered, outcome tags, book.
Dropped on a phone
✕Results tab. Reporting. Read it at the desk.
✕Settings tab. Its only content is the script, already one tap away in the header.
✕Score strip. Today's numbers are not why you opened the phone.
✕The kanban. Six 280px columns side by side becomes one stage at a time, picked from the chip row.
✕Docked cockpit. It stops squeezing next to the board and becomes the full screen sheet.
Folded away, one tap to open

The call button
✓Pinned to the bottom of the sheet, so it stays under your thumb while the outcomes scroll. Opens the lead in the CRM app, which dials from the client's business number and records the call, same rule as desktop.
›It tries the LeadConnector app first and falls back to the CRM in the browser if the app is not installed.

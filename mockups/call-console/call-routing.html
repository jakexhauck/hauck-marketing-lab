1

The phone layer

The audio. Nothing changes for how the team talks.

  • Customer calls the GHL number (the published line)
  • It forwards to whichever rep's cell
  • They answer on their phone, totally normal
  • Outbound: tap "Call" in the app, it dials their cell then bridges the customer
2

The app layer

The context + logging. This is the new part.

  • The GHL number reports every call to the app
  • App matches caller ID → known or new
  • Console pops with what they want + outcome buttons
  • One tap logs it and routes the lead
🔑
The one requirement that makes it all work
The number on the website, the truck, the Google listing has to be the GHL number forwarding to cells, not a rep's raw cell number. If calls go around the tracked number, the app never sees them. Route them through it, and everything else falls into place.
💻 At a desk

Console runs live, second screen

Cell on speaker or headset, the desktop shows the console during the call. Glance at what they want, tap the outcome as you wrap up.

📱 Out in the field

Console pops right after the call

Can't look at the app mid-call on one phone, so the moment the call ends the PWA surfaces the same one-tap outcome card. Log it in two seconds, keep moving.

Two layers, one tracked number in the middle · the audio stays on their phone, the data comes to the app

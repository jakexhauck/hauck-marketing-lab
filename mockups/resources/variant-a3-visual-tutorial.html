How online ads actually work

Every ad campaign is the same six-step machine. We'll use a window-cleaning client as the example. The same machine runs for a plumber, a pizza shop, or a personal trainer.

What you'll learn
  • 01The six pieces every campaign is built from — no matter the spend.
  • 02What a $10/day funnel actually looks like, drop by drop.
  • 03Real 30-day numbers from a live client. $312 in. $4,200 out.
Section 01

The six pieces of any ad campaign

Memorise these six pieces. You will never feel lost on a strategy call again. Every campaign — $10/day or $10,000/day — is built from the same parts.

01
Budget
You pay Meta to put your ad in front of people. Think of it as paying someone to deliver flyers.
02
Targeting
You pick who sees the ad. Location, age, interests. Meta does the heavy lifting from there.
03
Creative
The ad itself — image, video, headline, copy. This is the flyer that lands in front of the customer.
04
Click
When someone taps the ad. Most won't. Roughly 2–3% will. That's normal.
05
Conversion
The thing you actually want. A call. A booking. A form fill. A sale.
06
ROAS
Revenue divided by spend. The only number that decides if the machine is working.
Key takeaway
Six pieces. Every ad account. Forever. If a metric isn't one of these, it doesn't matter yet.
Section 02

What the $10/day funnel actually looks like

Most people won't click. Most clicks won't convert. That's not a problem — it's the shape of the funnel. Every dollar narrows as it moves down.

$10
Tier 01 · Spend
One day of budget. The entire machine starts here.
1,000
Tier 02 · Impressions
People who see the ad scroll past it on Facebook or Instagram.
20
Tier 03 · Clicks
Roughly 2% tap the ad. They land on the offer page.
4
Tier 04 · Leads
They fill the form or call the number. Now they're contactable.
1
Tier 05 · Customer
One paying job. Worth $80–$200 to a local service business.
Key takeaway
If one customer is worth $80+, that's $80 back on $10 spent. An 8× return — from a single day.

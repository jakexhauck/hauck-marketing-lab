How online ads actually work

Stripped to its bones, every ad campaign is the same six-step machine. We'll use a pizza shop as the example, but the same machine runs for a window cleaner, a plumber, or any other local business.

The six pieces of any ad campaign

Every campaign — whether you spend $10/day or $10,000/day — is made of the same six pieces. Memorise these and you'll never feel lost on a strategy call.

1. Budget

You pay Meta (Facebook + Instagram's parent company) to show ads. Think of it as paying someone to put flyers on doors.

2. Targeting

You pick who sees the ad — location, age, interests. Meta does most of the heavy lifting from there.

3. Creative

The ad itself — image, video, headline, text. This is the flyer.

4. Click

When someone taps the ad. Most people won't. That's normal.

5. Conversion

When someone does the thing you actually want — calls, books, or fills out a form.

6. ROAS

What you got back vs. what you spent. The whole point of running ads.

What the funnel looks like on $10/day

Most of the people who see the ad won't click. Most who click won't convert. That's the shape of the funnel, not a problem to fix.

$10spent on the day
500–1,000people see the ad impressions
15–25tap the ad clicks
3–5turn into leads
1–2become paying customers
Bottom line
If each customer is worth $40+, that's $80+ back on $10 spent. An 8x return.

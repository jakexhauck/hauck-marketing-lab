Five website directions

Every direction shares the same conversion block: the split-form card with the LED services board beneath it. Everything around it changes.

  • D.01

    The Operator's Console

    The site as a growth operations room. Scroll-tracked LED rail, system modules, Lexend type. You are looking over the operator's shoulder.

  • D.02

    The Plain-English Memo

    A typeset letter from Jake to every local owner. Source Serif 4, one column, zero noise. Confidence through restraint.

  • D.03

    Before and After

    Every section is a split: your week now vs your week with Hauck. A master switch flips the lights on. Atkinson Hyperlegible body.

  • D.04

    The Showroom

    Services staged like premium hardware under spotlights, with engraved spec plates. Hanken Grotesk throughout.

  • D.05

    One Lead's Journey

    Scrollytelling: one lead followed from ad click to five-star review along a single LED line. Timestamps carry the story.

## Decisions

- **Format:** 1920x1080, ~105s, AI voiceover (Kokoro, local), captions in the bottom band, quiet royalty-free music bed. A 9:16 cut follows after the master is approved.
- **Spine:** a chapter rail pinned top-left (Computer · Phone · iPhone · Android). The active chapter fills green. It never leaves the screen during the steps, so a viewer always knows where they are and can skip.
- **Hero prop:** the green "H" home-screen icon. Shown in the opening, it lands on each phone's home screen in frames 06 and 08, and it closes the film.
- **Held frame:** frame 10, the lock screen. Nothing moves while the line lands, then three notifications drop in.
- **Truthfulness:** login screens are rebuilt from the live capture of app.hauckmarketing.com/login (desktop + 390px phone). The notification card copy and button labels come from `NotificationPrompt.tsx`. Notification titles come from `functions/lib/push.ts`. iOS and Android system screens (share sheet, Chrome menu, permission dialogs) are simplified, faithful rebuilds. No real client data.
- **Bans:** see `frame.md`.

Built 2026-10-10 as 7 scenes (frames 2+3, 5+6, 7+8 of the v1 plan merged so each device keeps one continuous phone). Backdrop, chapter rail and captions are their own sub-compositions (`compositions/bg.html`, `rail.html`, `captions.html`, generated by `.work/build-index.mjs`).

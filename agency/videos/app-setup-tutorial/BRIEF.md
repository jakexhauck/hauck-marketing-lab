## Intent

How-to video for clients of the Hauck Marketing software at app.hauckmarketing.com.
One video, four chapters: log in on PC, log in on phone, add to home screen and
turn on notifications on iPhone, same on Android. Calm, plain language, the
viewer is a business owner, not a developer.

## Customizations

- AI voiceover (local Kokoro, free) with on-screen captions and a quiet royalty-free music bed.
- Chapter titles so viewers can skip to their device.
- After the 16:9 master is approved, build a 9:16 vertical cut (phones fill the frame, laptop chapter zooms onto the login box).

## Notes

- Visuals are rebuilt mockups of the real screens, never screen recordings, never real client data.
- Real login screen: label "Email" (placeholder name@email.com), label "Password" (placeholder "Enter password"), Sign in button.
- Real notification card: bell icon, title "Turn on notifications", buttons "Turn on" and "Not now".
- iPhone must add to the home screen first, or notifications are not offered. Steps from the app: Share button (square with arrow up) > "Add to Home Screen" > "Add" > open Hauck from home screen > Turn on > Allow.
- Android: Chrome ⋮ menu > "Add to Home screen" / "Install app" > open Hauck > Turn on > Allow.
- Home screen icon name is "Hauck".
- Login line: "Use the email and password we sent you."
- No support phone number given: closing card carries no phone number.
- Brand: green #4DBB83 on #0A1D19, Poppins display + Inter body.
- Never use em dashes in any on-screen text or narration.

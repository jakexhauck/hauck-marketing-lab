# AI b-roll prompts (Higgsfield)

Source: Jake's "AI Broll Prompt" SOP (agency Drive, Client SOPS), plus what worked on Clear Choice
Windows (Oct 2026). Every clip must read like a real worker filmed their crew on a phone.

## Settings (state these with every batch)

- Model `kling3_0_turbo`, 5s, 9:16, 1080p: **10 credits/clip**. Batch with `generate_video_batch`
  (up to 12), `use_unlim: false`. Seedance 2.5 costs 35 (720p) / 80 (1080p) per clip; not worth it.
- No audio is needed: the edit mutes b-roll.
- Starter plan has ~100 credits (too few). Plus plan = 1,200/month. Check `balance` first.

## Formula

- Open with exactly: `Handheld iPhone video, natural slight camera movement, filmed by a worker on the job.`
- Then the scene, about 45 words in total.
- Close with exactly: `Slightly grainy, real phone footage look, gentle handheld drift, no tripod steadiness.`
- POV variant: open `Chest-mounted action camera POV, wide-angle, filmed by a worker on the job.` (or
  `Head-mounted ... filmed by a worker on a ladder.`), close `Slightly grainy, real footage look, gentle movement.`
- Drone variant: open `Drone video, slow orbit, filmed by the crew.` (or `slow descending orbit`,
  `slow pull-back`), close `Slightly grainy, real footage look, gentle drift.`

## Hard rules

- Nobody looks at or talks to the camera; action already under way when the clip starts.
- Crew in **matching work outfits** (Jake's call): e.g. `matching dark green work shirts, tool belts and
  work gloves`. No readable logos or brand names on gear: AI cannot draw a real logo reliably.
- No vehicles, arrivals, handshakes or customers. Only hands-on work or finished-result showcases.
- Age old things by date, never adjective: `wood double-hung window from around 1975`, not "old worn".
- Slow pans and showcases need `well-lit` / `sunny`, or the result is muddy.
- Every clip must read in under a second to a homeowner. Cut trade-insider shots (e.g. "inside the
  triple pane glass").
- Ground it in the client's real job: house type, siding, window style, region (read the client's
  vault Brain/Profile first).

## Shot categories (cover all of them, and lean hard on crew shots)

1. Hook / problem: something visibly wrong (frost, a draft, rust, the old window).
2. Prep / removal: old thing coming out.
3. The work: the core install, hands and crew visible, ideally 2-4 people.
4. Proof: measure, level, test, lock.
5. Showcase / finishing: caulk, trim, wipe, sweep, crew packing up.
6. Payoff: the finished house, drone or wide pull-back.

A good ad needs about 25 clips (two batches). Fewer means repeats at ~0.7s cuts.

One brand.

Each direction is a complete four-page site: Home, Product, Shop and Our Story. All five use the same locked brand system from the guide, the same copy, and the same two product photographs. What changes is weight, framing and where the photography sits. Pick the one that feels right and it becomes the Shopify theme.

Parchment

Illustration-led

Cream dominates and photography steps back. The etched botanical language becomes structural: arched frames, double-ruled panels, ornament in the margins. Even the footer is cream instead of green. The quietest of the five, and the most like walking into an old apothecary.

Best if the client wants calm and heritage over commercial punch.

Deep Forest

Field Guide

Editorial almanac

Golden Hour

Photography-forward

What every direction fixes

  • Two products no longer look like an empty store. No direction uses a plain two-card grid. Each solves it differently, and the Shop page adds a bundle so two SKUs become three offers.
  • The hero stops competing with the product shots. The hero photo appears once per site; product images get shaped or framed so they read as objects.
  • Social proof exists. Every direction has a review band.
  • The story is on the site. North Georgia origin plus real ingredient detail on every product page.
  • Sections have rhythm. Each direction commits to one dominant section type instead of centring everything.

Read before you send it on

  • Only the two product photographs are real assets. No other image from the brand folder is used anywhere.
  • Review quotes and star counts are placeholder. They must be swapped for real reviews before this goes anywhere near a customer.
  • The pouches are not cut out. Backgrounds are handled with shaped crops and framing. If you want true cutouts on cream, that is a quick pass in Photoshop and the images drop straight in.
  • The 40-bag size, the subscription option and the bundle price are invented to show the layout. Real merchandising decisions are the client's.
  • These are HTML mockups, not a Shopify theme. Every section maps one-to-one to a Shopify section when you pick a winner.

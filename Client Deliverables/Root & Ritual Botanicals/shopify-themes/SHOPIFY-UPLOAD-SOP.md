# Root & Ritual Botanicals: Shopify Theme Upload SOP

**A tick-box guide. Work top to bottom.**

Prepared by Hauck Marketing. Every step below was checked against the Shopify Help Center on 26 July 2026.

Total time: about 90 minutes, most of it typing product copy.

---

## The short version

1. Upload all five zips as drafts. (10 min)
2. Preview all five. Pick one. (15 min)
3. Add your products, pages and menus. (45 min)
4. Connect the theme to them. (15 min)
5. Run the pre-launch checks. (10 min)
6. Publish.

**Nothing you do before step 6 can affect a live site.** Drafts are private.

---

Five separate themes, one per design direction. Each is a complete, installable theme.

## Stage 0: Before you touch anything

- [ ] **Confirm you can edit themes.** You need to be the store owner, or a staff member with the **Themes** permission. Without it the Themes page is read only and no upload button appears.

- [ ] **Confirm the Online Store channel is on.** Look for **Online Store** in the left sidebar. If it is missing, add it under **Settings** then **Sales channels**.

- [ ] **Check you have room for five more themes.** Your limit depends on your plan.

| Plan | Theme limit |
|---|---|
| Starter | Cannot upload themes at all |
| Basic, Grow, Advanced | 20 |
| Plus | 100 |

If you are at the limit, delete an old draft first.

- [ ] **Do not unzip the files.** Upload them exactly as delivered. Unzipping and rezipping on a Mac adds a hidden `__MACOSX` folder that makes the upload fail.

---

## Stage 1: Upload all five

Do this five times, once per zip.

- [ ] Go to **Online Store** then **Themes**.
- [ ] Scroll down to the **Draft themes** section. It sits below your published theme.
- [ ] Click **Import theme**, then **Upload zip file**.
- [ ] In the dialog, click **Choose File**.
- [ ] Pick one zip.
- [ ] Click **Upload**.
- [ ] Wait. It usually takes under a minute.

The theme appears in **Draft themes** named after its direction, for example "Root & Ritual - Deep Forest".

**Repeat for all five zips.**

- [ ] All five now show in the Draft themes list.

> **On a phone?** Same sequence in the Shopify app: **Menu** then **Online Store** then **Manage themes** then **Import theme**.

## Stage 2: Preview and pick one

You are choosing a direction here, not judging content. The products are not connected yet, so the placeholder copy and stock photos are expected.

- [ ] Find a theme in **Draft themes**.
- [ ] Click the **menu button** (the three dots) next to it.
- [ ] Click **Preview**.
- [ ] A new tab opens showing your store with that theme. Your live site is untouched and customers see nothing.
- [ ] Use the preview bar at the bottom to move around.
- [ ] Repeat for all five.

- [ ] **Decision made. Direction chosen:** ................................

Everything from here applies to the one you picked.

> **What to actually look at:** where the story sits, how the product rows are built, whether the navigation is in the header or behind a menu button. Those are the real differences. Ignore the placeholder text.

### What is genuinely different between them

Not colour. Structure.

| Direction | Character | Built differently |
|---|---|---|
| **Faithful** | The original comp, rebuilt | Split hero, standard header. The safe pick. |
| **Parchment** | Cream, illustrated, calm | Arched photo frames, botanical plate caption, story pulled high up the page |
| **Deep Forest** | Green carries the page | Hero closes with a trust band, trust marks sit directly under the fold |
| **Field Guide** | Editorial almanac | Centred masthead under a dated rule, specs as ruled data tables, specimen numbers |
| **Golden Hour** | Photography forward | Full-height hero, products as edge-to-edge bands with copy over the photo, navigation hidden behind a menu button |

Each also orders the homepage differently:

| Direction | Homepage order |
|---|---|
| Faithful | hero, intro, products, icons, story, reviews |
| Parchment | hero, intro, product, **story**, product, icons, reviews |
| Deep Forest | hero, **icons**, intro, products, story, reviews |
| Field Guide | hero, intro, **story**, products, icons, reviews |
| Golden Hour | hero, **products**, intro, story, icons, reviews |

You can still reorder any of it in **Customize** if you disagree with the call.

## Stage 3: Build the store content

The theme has no idea what your products or pages are yet. Do these in order.

### 3a. The two products

- [ ] Go to **Products** then **Add product**.
- [ ] Create **Morning Ritual**. Set your real price. Upload the product photo.
- [ ] Write the real copy in the **Description** field.
- [ ] Save.
- [ ] Repeat for **Evening Ritual**.

> **Why no price is showing yet:** until a product is connected, the theme shows no price at all. That is deliberate. It will never display a number it made up, so an unfinished store cannot quote a price you never agreed to.

### 3b. The collection

- [ ] Go to **Products** then **Collections** then **Create collection**.
- [ ] Name it **The Collection**.
- [ ] Set **Collection type** to **Manual**.
- [ ] Add both products.
- [ ] Save.

### 3c. The pages

**Our Story**

- [ ] **Online Store** then **Pages** then **Add page**.
- [ ] Title: **Our Story**.
- [ ] Paste in your story copy.
- [ ] Leave **Theme template** on **page**.
- [ ] Save.

**Contact**

- [ ] **Add page**. Title: **Contact**.
- [ ] Under **Theme template**, choose **contact**.
- [ ] Save.

> **This one matters.** On the default `page` template you get your text and no form. The **contact** template is what turns it into a working contact form.
>
> Messages arrive at the address set under **Settings** then **Notifications**. Check that address is one you actually read.

**FAQ**

- [ ] **Add page**. Title: **Questions** (or **FAQ**).
- [ ] Under **Theme template**, choose **faq**.
- [ ] Save.
- [ ] The theme ships four sample questions. You will edit them in stage 4.

### 3d. The menus

- [ ] Go to **Online Store** then **Navigation**.
- [ ] Open **Main menu**. Set it to: Home, Shop (link to your collection), Our Story, Contact.
- [ ] Open **Footer menu**. Add your policy and info links.
- [ ] Save.

> Every footer link now lands on a real page. Nothing dead ends.

## Stage 4: Connect the theme to your content

- [ ] Go to **Online Store** then **Themes**.
- [ ] On your chosen draft, click **Customize**.

### 4a. The product rows

- [ ] In the left panel, click the first **Product feature row**.
- [ ] In **Product**, select **Morning Ritual**.
- [ ] The title, price, description and photo now pull from the real product automatically.
- [ ] Click the second **Product feature row** and select **Evening Ritual**.
- [ ] Click **Save**.

### 4b. The bundle (skip if you are not selling one)

The Bundle offer section sits on the collection page. It invents nothing. It shows the real price of a real bundle product and works the saving out from the products you tell it are inside.

- [ ] First, in Shopify, create a product for the set itself, for example **The Full Ritual Set**, with its own price.
- [ ] Back in **Customize**, open the collection page.
- [ ] Click **Bundle offer**.
- [ ] In **Bundle product**, choose the set. That price is what customers see.
- [ ] In the two **Included product** blocks, choose Morning Ritual and Evening Ritual.
- [ ] Check the **Save X%** badge that appears. It is calculated from those prices. If the number looks wrong, your prices are wrong.
- [ ] Save.

> Leave **Bundle product** empty and the section shows no price and no saving. Nothing is invented to fill the gap.

### 4c. The FAQ

- [ ] Navigate to your FAQ page inside **Customize**.
- [ ] Click the **FAQ** section.
- [ ] Replace the four sample questions with yours.
- [ ] Save.

### 4d. The announcement bar

- [ ] Click **Announcement bar** at the top of the section list.
- [ ] It currently reads "Free shipping on orders over $50".
- [ ] Change it or make it match your real shipping policy.
- [ ] Save.

## Stage 5: Pre-launch checks

**Do not skip this stage.**

### 5a. Reviews. The one that matters.

- [ ] In **Customize**, click the **Reviews** section.
- [ ] All three blocks currently read "PLACEHOLDER - replace with a real customer review before launch."
- [ ] Replace each with a genuine quote and a real name.
- [ ] **If you have no reviews yet, delete the whole Reviews section.** Do not invent any.
- [ ] Save.

> Fake reviews breach Shopify's terms and, in the US, FTC rules. This is the one thing on this page that can actually cost you money.

### 5b. Buy something

- [ ] Open the preview.
- [ ] Go to a product page.
- [ ] Change the variant option. Confirm the price, the photo and the Add to Cart button all update.
- [ ] Add it to the cart.
- [ ] Go to the cart and start checkout.
- [ ] Confirm the right product, right variant and right price.

> Nobody has done this yet. The theme was built and tested against an empty store. You are the first person to point it at a real catalogue.

### 5c. Click every link

- [ ] Every item in the main menu goes somewhere real.
- [ ] Every item in the footer goes somewhere real.
- [ ] The Contact page shows a form, not just text.
- [ ] Send yourself a test message through it and confirm it arrives.

### 5d. Look at it on a phone

- [ ] Open the preview on your actual phone, not a browser resized on your desktop.
- [ ] Check the homepage, a product page, and the cart.

## Stage 6: Publish

- [ ] Go to **Online Store** then **Themes**.
- [ ] Find your chosen theme in **Draft themes**.
- [ ] Click **Publish**.
- [ ] Confirm.

It is live.

Your previous theme drops down into the draft list, unchanged.

### If something is wrong: rolling back

- [ ] Go to **Online Store** then **Themes**.
- [ ] Find the previous theme in **Draft themes**.
- [ ] Click **Publish**.

That is the whole rollback. About five seconds. Nothing is lost.

## Stage 7: Tidy up

- [ ] Click the **menu button** next to a theme you did not choose.
- [ ] Click **Remove**.
- [ ] Confirm.
- [ ] Repeat for the other three.

**Never remove the published theme.**

> Keep one runner-up as a draft for a month if you are undecided. It costs nothing.

## Read this before you promise anyone customer accounts

Shopify changed how accounts work, and it is not a theme decision any more.

**Legacy customer accounts were deprecated on 26 February 2026.** They are no longer available to new stores, and Shopify has said a final shut-off date will be announced later in 2026.

What that means for you:

- **If your store uses the current customer accounts** (all new stores do), the login and account pages are hosted by Shopify. They will not match this theme's styling, and no theme can change that. This is now normal across every Shopify store.
- **If your store is still on legacy accounts**, the theme's own account pages are used and they do match the design.

Either way it works. The difference is only whether the account screens look like the rest of the site.

- [ ] Check which one you are on under **Settings** then **Customer accounts**.
- [ ] If you are on the current version, do not promise the client branded login screens.

## Troubleshooting

**"There was an error uploading your theme"**
The zip was probably unzipped and rezipped. Use the original file exactly as delivered.

**There is no Import theme button**
Either the Online Store channel is off, or your staff account lacks the **Themes** permission. Both are fixed in Settings.

**The upload worked but the store looks unstyled**
Hard refresh. Ctrl+F5 on Windows, Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac. Shopify caches stylesheets hard.

**Icons show as empty squares**
`icons.js` did not load. In the theme editor open **Assets** and confirm `icons.js` is there. If an app on your store blocks or defers theme scripts, allow this one.

**The homepage shows placeholder products, or no prices**
You have not connected the products yet. See stage 4a. The missing price is intentional until then.

**The Contact page shows my text but no form**
Wrong template. Edit the page and set **Theme template** to **contact**.

**Changing the variant does nothing**
JavaScript is blocked or failed to load. The picker falls back to a plain dropdown that still adds the correct product, so nothing is broken, it just looks plainer.

**I published the wrong one**
See "rolling back" in stage 6. It is instant.

## Known limitations, stated plainly

1. **Reviews ship as placeholder.** Covered in stage 5a. Replace or delete.

2. **Fonts load from Google, not Shopify's CDN.** This is the only warning Shopify's own theme checker raises, seven times per theme. It costs a little page speed. Self hosting the fonts is a small follow-up job if you want a perfect score.

3. **The two product photos are bundled inside each theme** as fallbacks, so the store never looks broken. Real product images take over automatically once uploaded.

4. **Golden Hour stretches the photos past their native size.** Those images are 1254 pixels square and that direction runs them edge to edge. It holds up today, but that direction rewards a proper photo shoot at 2400 pixels or wider more than the other four do.

5. **Customer account screens may not match the design.** See the section above. This is a Shopify platform change, not a gap in the build.

6. **No one has run these against a real catalogue yet.** Verified with Shopify's `theme check` (zero errors on all five) and rendered locally against an empty store, which is the state you upload in. Stage 5b is where that gets proven.

## What you can change yourself

All of this is a setting in **Customize**, not a code change:

- Every heading, paragraph and button label
- All images
- The announcement bar
- Section order on any page
- Which sections appear at all
- Colours and fonts, under **Theme settings**

Anything structural, like a new page type or a redesigned blog, comes back to us.

Mountain medicine
for modern life.

Root & Ritual was born in the North Georgia mountains. We believe healing starts slowly.

There is a stretch of ridge in North Georgia where the fog sits in the valley until nearly ten in the morning. If you grew up there, you know the herbs that come off that ridge: self-heal along the fence line, lemon balm gone feral behind the shed, chamomile that reseeds itself every spring whether you asked it to or not.

Our grandmother did not call it wellness. She called it Tuesday. A cup of something bitter in the morning to get the blood moving. A cup of something soft at night so the day would let go of you.

We are not interested in the quick fix.

Most of what gets sold as wellness is a promise that something will change fast. That is not how any of this works, and we think you already know that.

What actually changes people is repetition. The same cup, at the same hour, until the body starts to recognise the signal. That is the whole idea behind both blends: not a supplement, a ritual.

How we make it.

Small batches, blended by hand. Whole-cut ginger, never powdered, because powdered ginger tastes like dust and you can tell. Real lavender buds, not lavender flavouring. Prunella vulgaris in both blends, because it is the plant this place gave us and we are not going to leave it out to save money.

Twenty pyramid bags to a pouch. Kraft, unbleached, recyclable. Nothing in the box that does not need to be in the box.

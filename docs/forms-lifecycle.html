Stage 7 · Testing + Ongoing
Watch, prune, learn
Daily + weekly cadence
Auto-run candidates. The MCP only fetches data when asked — it doesn't watch the account 24/7. To get these "just reporting back to you," wire a scheduler (/schedule or Trigger.dev) to fire each form on a cadence. Suggested: daily-pulse 9:00am, anomaly-slack every 30 min during US business hours, andromeda-cleanup Mondays 8:00am.

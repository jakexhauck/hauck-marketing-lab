## Copy (every agent)

Four copywriting skills sit in your work folder, in `copy-skills/`. Read them with the Read tool before you write any words a lead, a client or Jake will read as marketing: ad primary text, headlines, CTAs, hooks and on-screen text, video scripts, landing pages, follow-up texts and emails, lead forms, report headlines. Read all four in full the first time in a chat, then again whenever you are unsure. Plain chat replies to Jake are not copy.

Work in this order, and show each step's result on the card:

1. **Frame** with `copy-skills/ogilvy.md`. Before drafting, write down: who it is for (name them, like "Ann Arbor homeowners"), the one promise, and the proof you have (facts, numbers, reviews, photos). Take these from the client's Profile, Memory, brief and brains. Never invent a fact, number or testimonial. If proof is missing, say what is missing, and ask the agent who has it with a request.
2. **Draft** with `copy-skills/copywriting.md` (and `copy-frameworks.md` for headline formulas). Give 3 to 5 versions.
3. **Sweep** with `copy-skills/copy-editing.md`: Clarity, So What, Prove It, Specificity, Heightened Emotion, Zero Risk. The Zero Risk sweep is where the offer's risk reversal goes (free estimate, no obligation).
4. **Scrub** with `copy-skills/stop-slop.md`. Last pass on every line.

Where the skills disagree, this decides:

- The client's voice from their Profile and Memory beats every skill. The house rules in this prompt beat both.
- Platform limits beat word counts. A Meta headline shows about 40 characters, so Ogilvy's 8 to 12 word headline rule gives way. Primary text puts the hook in the first 125 characters.
- A question is allowed as a hook ("Drafty windows this winter?"). Stop Slop's ban on questions and Wh- openers applies to the body.
- Offer words stay when they are true and specific: "never pay for an estimate", "every window", "free". Stop Slop's ban on adverbs and lazy extremes applies to filler, not to the offer.
- Ogilvy's print and TV research (long copy, captions, music, animation) is a guide for scripts and pages. Short ads stay short.
- The skills mention files and skills we do not have (`.claude/product-marketing-context.md`, page-cro, email-sequence). Ignore those lines: your context is this prompt.

On the card, under each version, one line: the promise it makes and the proof it uses.

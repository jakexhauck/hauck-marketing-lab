# Social Media: Services Map (What We Could Offer & Beat GHL At)

> Strategy map only. No build instructions. Decide what's worth it, then we plan the "how" separately.
> Lens: local service-business clients (the kind we run Meta ads for). Our edge = AI voice cloning (copywriter), revenue/lead attribution (we own the CRM + GHL backend), and paid-social expertise (Jake runs Meta ads daily).

---

## The three tiers

- **Tier 1 - Table stakes.** GHL already does it natively. We either surface theirs or skip it. No moat here.
- **Tier 2 - Differentiators.** GHL is weak or generic. This is where we win clients and justify the price.
- **Tier 3 - Things GHL structurally can't do.** Our unfair advantages because we own the whole stack (ads + CRM + AI voice).

---

## Tier 1 - Table stakes (match or surface, don't reinvent)

These are commodities. Clients expect them. Rebuilding them from scratch is wasted effort; better to surface GHL's or wrap them lightly.

| Service | GHL native | Our move |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-platform publishing (FB, IG, GBP, LinkedIn, TikTok, X, Pinterest, YouTube) | Yes | Surface GHL's, clean up the UI |
| Calendar / scheduling / queue slots | Yes | Surface, improve the view |
| Bulk CSV scheduling | Yes | Surface |
| Reels / Stories / carousels / first comment | Yes | Surface |
| RSS auto-posting | Yes | Skip (low value for local biz) |
| Evergreen content categories / recycling | Yes | Surface |
| Basic approval step | Yes (clunky) | Replace, see Tier 2 |

**Takeaway:** Don't sell "we post to social." Everyone does. It's the floor, not the offer.

---

## Tier 2 - Differentiators (where GHL is weak and we win)

### 1. AI that writes in the client's actual voice
- **GHL gap:** Generic AI caption generator. Same bland tone for a roofer and a med-spa. No memory of the brand.
- **Us:** The `copywriter` skill builds a real voice profile per client (from their existing posts + Profile/Memory vault). Captions sound like *them*, not ChatGPT.
- **Why it sticks:** This is the #1 reason agencies lose social clients ("it doesn't sound like us"). We solve it structurally.

### 2. Real analytics that a business owner understands
- **GHL gap:** Vanity metrics (reach, likes) with no "so what."
- **Us:** Plain-English reporting (we already have the `data-analyst` skill). "This post drove 4 calls." "Tuesday 7pm is your best slot." "Reels are outperforming photos 3:1, do more."
- **Why it sticks:** Owners don't care about impressions. They care about the phone ringing.

### 3. Content approval that doesn't suck
- **GHL gap:** Approval exists but is clunky and buried.
- **Us:** A clean approval inbox. Client sees the post mockup as it'll actually look on each platform, taps approve/edit/reject, leaves a comment. Mobile-first (clients live on their phone).
- **Why it sticks:** Removes the #1 friction in agency-client social: the back-and-forth.

### 4. A content engine, not a blank box
- **GHL gap:** GHL gives you an empty composer. The client/agency still has to think of what to post.
- **Us:** Per-industry content frameworks + a monthly content plan generated from the client's services, seasonality, and offers. "Here are 20 posts for June, approve the batch." Turns a blank page into a one-tap month.
- **Why it sticks:** The hardest part of social isn't posting, it's deciding what to post. We remove that.

### 5. Repurposing pipeline
- **GHL gap:** None. One post = one platform format.
- **Us:** One idea auto-adapted per platform (long caption for FB, punchy for IG, professional for LinkedIn, hook-first for TikTok). Plus turning a single review or job photo into a full post.
- **Why it sticks:** 5x the output from the same input.

### 6. Visual / brand consistency
- **GHL gap:** Image generation exists but is off-brand and generic.
- **Us:** Brand kit per client (colors, fonts, logo placement). Templates so every post looks on-brand. Pull from the client's own job photos.
- **Why it sticks:** Local businesses look amateur on social. Consistent visuals = looks like a real brand.

## Tier 3 - Unfair advantages (GHL structurally can't do this)

These only work because we own the ads + CRM + AI stack. No standalone social tool can copy them.

### 1. Revenue attribution (the killer feature)
- We own the CRM and the GHL backend. We can tie an organic post to a lead to a booked job to revenue.
- "This Facebook post generated 3 leads worth $14k in pipeline."
- No social scheduler on earth can say this. This alone justifies a premium price.

### 2. Organic-to-paid bridge (Jake's Meta ads edge)
- See which organic posts perform, then one-tap boost the winners as Meta ads (we already run the ad accounts).
- Use organic engagement data to inform paid targeting and creative.
- Feed top-performing ad creative back into the organic calendar.
- We're the only ones running both sides, so we can close this loop.

### 3. Social fed by CRM events
- New 5-star review comes in -> auto-draft a "look what our client said" post for approval. (Ties into the Google Reviews build already planned.)
- Job completed -> draft a before/after post from the job photos.
- Hit a milestone (100th job, anniversary) -> draft a celebration post.
- The CRM is the content firehose. GHL can't connect these because they don't own your client's job data the way we do.

### 4. Lead capture inside social
- DMs and comments that look like leads get pulled into the CRM as contacts automatically, routed to the right pipeline.
- Auto-reply to comment/DM intent ("DM us for a quote") -> creates a lead, triggers the follow-up automation we already built.
- Closes the gap between "got engagement" and "got a customer."

### 5. Unified inbox that's actually unified
- **GHL gap:** Social comments/DMs are half-baked, separate from the main conversations inbox.
- **Us:** Social DMs/comments land in the same conversation history as SMS/email/calls (we already built channel-scoped history). One thread per human across every channel.

## Cross-cutting ideas (apply across tiers)

- **Competitor watch:** Track 3-5 local competitors' posting cadence and top content. "Your competitor posts 4x/week, you post 1x." Pure motivation + strategy fuel. GHL has nothing here.
- **Best-time-to-post, learned per account:** Not generic best-practice times, but learned from *this* client's actual engagement.
- **Hashtag/keyword intelligence per local market:** Local-relevant tags, not generic.
- **Employee/owner advocacy:** Easy way for the owner to share posts to their personal profile (huge for local trust).
- **UGC collection:** Prompt happy customers to send photos/videos, funnel into the content engine.

## How to think about packaging (not a build plan, just framing)

- **Floor (Tier 1):** "We run your social." Table stakes, low price, commodity.
- **Core offer (Tier 2):** "AI in your voice + content you actually approve + reporting you understand." This is the sellable middle.
- **Premium (Tier 3):** "Your social, ads, and CRM as one revenue engine." This is the moat and the upsell. Nobody else can offer it because nobody else owns all three.

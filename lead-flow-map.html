The rule that keeps it clean

  • One opportunity per lead. Born on a capture page, moves into the Sales Pipeline at its handoff stage. Never copied.
  • The source tag never changes. A paid-ad lead stays "paid ad" even after it's a job, so attribution always holds.
  • Capture pages filter by source. Each shows its own full pipeline; they keep their lead in view after handoff.
  • Sales pages filter by stage. Intro Calls / Estimates / Jobs are three windows on one pipeline.

# 12 Ad Angles

## Core principle
**ONE AD = ONE REASON TO BUY.** Each ad targets a different motivation, not variations of the same headline. If you're testing 10 ads, they should attack 10 different psychological levers, not be 10 rewrites of the same offer.

## The 12 angles (Tony's Pizza example, translates to any niche)

| # | Angle | Lever |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1 | Too Tired to Cook | Pain: exhaustion after work |
| 2 | Family Friday | Desire: quality time together |
| 3 | Half the Price | Logic: value comparison |
| 4 | Real Italian | Quality: authenticity |
| 5 | 20-Min Delivery | Convenience: speed |
| 6 | 400+ 5-Star Reviews | Social proof: trust |
| 7 | New in the Area | Discovery: local targeting |
| 8 | Party Catering | Occasion: events |
| 9 | Healthy Option | Health: guilt-free choice |
| 10 | Easy Ordering | Convenience: simplicity |
| 11 | Late Night | Timing: night owls |
| 12 | Limited Special | Urgency: scarcity |

## How to use this
1. Pick the client's niche.
2. Translate each of the 12 levers into a niche-specific angle.
3. Write one ad per angle. Different angle = different ad, even if the offer is the same.
4. Pair each angle with the framework that fits it best (see [[ad-frameworks]]).

## Angle → framework pairing cheat-sheet
- Pain levers (#1, #11) → PAS
- Offers/value levers (#3, #5, #12) → AIDA
- Transformation levers (#2, #9) → BAB
- Trust/story levers (#6, #4, #7) → STORY

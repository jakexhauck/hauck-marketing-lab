# 6 AI Anti-Patterns to Kill on Sight

Every AI draft has these. Strip them out before any ad goes live.

## 1. "Not X, It's Y" contrast (AI loves em dashes here)
- Bad: "This isn't just pizza, it's an experience"
- Good: "Real Italian dough. 20-minute delivery. $12.99"

## 2. Triple structure ("X. Y. And Z.")
- Bad: "Fresh ingredients. Fast delivery. Unbeatable prices"
- Good: "Our dough is made at 5am with imported Italian flour"

## 3. "Imagine this" opener
- Bad: "Imagine biting into a slice of perfectly crispy pizza..."
- Good: "I took one bite and said, 'this is $12.99?'"

## 4. Filler power words (elevate, transform, unlock, game-changer)
- Bad: "Elevate your dinner experience"
- Good: "My kids stopped fighting for 10 whole minutes"

## 5. Question-then-answer
- Bad: "Tired of cold pizza? Want real quality? Tony's has the answer"
- Good: "I used to think $35 delivery was normal. Then I found Tony's. $12.99"

## 6. Perfect grammar robot
- Bad: "We are proud to offer Curitiba's finest artisanal pizza"
- Good: "We've been making pizza 15 years. Imported flour, real tomatoes. Just order"

## The rule
> Read every AI-generated ad out loud. If you'd cringe hearing a real person say it in conversation, rewrite it.

# 60-Second Edit Process

Run this on every AI draft before it ships.

1. **Read aloud** — would you say this to a friend?
2. **Check hook** — first line = scroll stopper?
3. **Kill AI words** — elevate, transform, seamless, unlock → delete
4. **Add specifics** — "$12.99" beats "affordable", "417 reviews" beats "trusted"
5. **Match voice** — sound like the brand, not AI

# Universal Hauck Marketing rule
Never use em dashes anywhere. Not in chat, ad copy, emails, docs, UI, code comments. Use commas, periods, parentheses, or colons. (Encoded in vault About notes and CLAUDE.md.)

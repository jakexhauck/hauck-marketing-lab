# 4 Ad Copy Frameworks

The four core direct-response frameworks every ad should pick from. Match the framework to the audience and the angle.

## PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)
Best for: pain-point heavy audiences.

- P: State the pain
- A: Twist the knife (agitate)
- S: Present the fix

Example (Tony's Pizza, "too tired to cook" angle):
> It's 7pm. You just walked in the door. The kids are hungry. The fridge is empty. You could spend the next hour at the grocery store, 30 minutes cooking, 20 cleaning. Or scroll DoorDash 15 min and pay $40 for cold pizza. Or tap one button and have Tony's hot pizza at your door in 20 minutes. $12.99. Your kids will think you're a hero. Order now.

## AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action)
Best for: offers, deals, promotions.

- A: Attention, stop the scroll
- I: Interest, keep reading
- D: Desire, want it
- A: Action, click now

Example:
> The best pizza in Curitiba just dropped a new deal. Hand-tossed dough made fresh every morning. Imported San Marzano tomatoes. Mozzarella that actually stretches. Tony's has been doing this for 15 years, there's a reason the line wraps around the block Saturdays. This week: 2 large pizzas + garlic bread + 4 drinks = $19.99. That's dinner for the whole family, delivered hot to your door. Tap 'Order Now' before Friday. This deal disappears at midnight.

## BAB (Before-After-Bridge)
Best for: transformation stories.

- Before: Life now (sucks)
- After: Life with product
- Bridge: How to get there

Example:
> Before: It's Tuesday night. You're staring at the fridge. Leftover rice from 3 days ago. Half an onion. Ketchup. The kids are asking 'what's for dinner?' for the 4th time. After: Everyone's sitting around the table. Hot pizza, still steaming. The kids are fighting over the last slice of pepperoni. You didn't cook. You didn't clean. You just ordered. Bridge: Tony's Pizza delivers in 20 minutes. Real ingredients. Real crust. $12.99. Tap below and go from 'what's for dinner' to 'dinner's here' in the time it takes to set the table.

## STORY (Character-Conflict-Resolution)
Best for: emotional connection, UGC-style ads.

- Character: relatable person
- Conflict: the struggle
- Resolution: product saves day

Example:
> My friend Maria works two jobs. She's got 3 kids, a mortgage, zero time. Last week she called me almost crying. 'I forgot to go to the store again. The kids had cereal for dinner. I feel like the worst mom.' I told her, stop. You're not a bad mom. You're a busy mom. There's a difference. I sent her Tony's. She ordered from her phone while walking to her car. By the time she got home, hot pizza was at the door. Her 7-year-old said 'Mom you're the best.' $12.99 well spent. Busy moms, Tony's has your back. Order in 30 seconds.

## Copy lengths

- **Short (under 50 words)**: feed and story ads. Best for PAS pain hooks.
- **Medium (50-100 words)**: feed ads with "See More". Best for PAS family or STORY UGC.
- **Long (100+ words)**: high-intent, founder/origin storytelling.

> [!danger] Using a fake personal account to create BM
> Meta checks. They'll ban your BM and every ad account inside it. Use your real Facebook profile.

> [!danger] Creating multiple BMs to get around a ban
> Meta links them by IP, device, and payment method. They'll ban those too. If you get restricted, appeal, don't try to sneak around it.

> [!danger] Setting the wrong timezone or currency
> You can't change currency after creation. Wrong timezone means your daily budgets reset at the wrong time. Double check before clicking create.

> [!warning] Not enabling two-factor authentication (2FA)
> Go to Business Settings → Security Center → turn on 2FA for everyone. If someone hacks your BM, they can drain every ad account inside it.

> [!warning] Giving clients admin access to your BM
> Only give clients access to THEIR ad account and THEIR page. Never full BM admin. They could accidentally (or intentionally) mess up your other clients' stuff.

> [!tip] Best practice
> Add a second admin to your BM (a trusted partner, co-founder, or even a second email you own). If your main account gets locked out, the second admin can recover everything.

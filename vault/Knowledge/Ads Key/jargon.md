| Term | What it actually means |
| --- | --- |
| **Impression** | Someone saw your ad (scrolled past it) |
| **CTR (Click-Through Rate)** | % of people who tapped the ad. 2-5% is normal. |
| **CPM** | Cost per 1,000 views. How expensive it is to show your ad. $5-15 is normal for local. |
| **CPC (Cost Per Click)** | How much each tap costs you. $0.50-$3 is normal for local. |
| **CPL (Cost Per Lead)** | How much each phone call / form fill costs. $5-25 for local businesses. |
| **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)** | How much a paying customer costs you. This is the number that matters most. |
| **ROAS** | Return on ad spend. Spent $100, made $500 = 5x ROAS. Above 3x is good for most local businesses. |
| **Pixel** | A tracking code on the website that tells Meta "this person bought / called / booked." |
| **Learning Phase** | First 3-7 days where Meta figures out who responds to your ad. Results are messy. Don't panic. |

title: Learning Phase Framework
type: ads-key

---

1. **Launch 3-5 different ads to the same audience.** Different images/videos, different headlines, different angles. Keep the audience the same so you're only testing the creative.

2. **Wait for data, don't panic.** Let each ad spend at least 2-3x your target cost per lead before making any decisions. If your target CPL is $15, wait until each ad has spent $30-$45. This usually takes 3-5 days.

3. **Kill the losers.** If an ad has spent 3x your target CPL and still has zero or very few leads, turn it off. Don't hope it gets better. It won't. Redirect that budget to what's working.

4. **Scale the winners slowly.** Found an ad that's crushing it? Increase budget by 20-30% every 2-3 days. Going from $15/day to $20/day is fine. Going from $15/day to $50/day overnight will reset the learning phase and tank performance.

5. **Repeat forever.** Even winners fatigue eventually. When CPL starts rising, launch new creatives. The cycle never stops: test → kill losers → scale winners → test new creatives.

> [!danger] The #1 beginner mistake: changing things too fast
> You launch an ad, see no leads after 24 hours, and panic-edit the targeting. Meta needs 3-5 days to learn who to show your ad to (this is called the "learning phase"). Every time you edit the ad set, the learning phase resets. Be patient.

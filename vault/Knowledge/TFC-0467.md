# Raising The Clients Interest Level

## TL;DR
Media Buying Expert sales training on agency sales, closing, and high-ticket deals.

## Content
18. Raising The Clients Interest Level.mp4 You, as the salesperson, you have to have a sustained high level of conviction in order for a sale to occur. You cannot, in any way, shape, or form, capitulate to the other party's level, okay? So, you as the salesperson going into the deal. Your interest levels are going to be high. The potential client, they are going to have low interest levels, okay? So, to be clear, a deal happens when both parties' interests are peaked. So, you as the salesperson. Your interest levels have to be as high as they can possibly be, aka your conviction about your product. You're going to come across resistance, obviously, when you're talking to people. Now, to be clear, a client enters into the deal. They might have a little bit of interest. You know, maybe they have the problem that your product or service solves. Maybe they just have a general interest or curiosity around understanding what it is that you do. Maybe some of their friends bought your product and they're trying to figure out why people bought your product so they can justify it themselves. Well, watch this. The client's interest level comes in low, okay? So when you talk to somebody who naturally has a low interest level, and you have to get their interest level higher in order for a deal to actually occur, what does that mean? That means you cannot lower your interest level. You cannot lower your conviction rate to their level. You should be fully sold on why your product costs as much as it does, why it is the way it is, you know, and why it's going to be beneficial for the client. And when you are dealing with somebody who has a lower level of interest than yours, and you continue to explain yourself, feature by feature, overcoming objection by objection, what happens is, is one step at a time, you're moving the client's interest level up. And you likely recognize this. All of a sudden... Tonality changes in the individual. You know, you can hear it in their voice that there's a little more interest and a little more curiosity. You know, there's a little less resistance than there was at the beginning point of the conversation when their interest was at the lowest. Their interest will raise because your level of conviction and interest was sustained. If you lower your interest level, the client will lower their interest level. It is a game of capitulating to one another. When they enter, if you waiver on the bar of interest you have set, you will be pulled down to their level and no deal occurs. That a deal occurs is when you maintain your level of conviction about why the person needs that product, service, or offer. And in doing so, you will raise their level of interest in order for a deal to eventually occur. There is no other way a sale happens. A sale happens when both parties are at the same level of agreement with one another. Then a deal gets done. If you capitulate to their level that they enter into the deal with, you will not get a deal. Keep your conviction insanely high about what it is that you are selling and why the person on the other end of that phone needs it. Otherwise, once again, a deal won't occur.

Featured section Max 2. Deplatform to the free thing or make it go right to the website or download app. Pose the benefits and end results here. High-performing content set-up for videos, text posts, carousels, stories, you name it. Hook/Trailer: Bring them in with curiosity, a big promise, or a lesson learned. Break the scroll pattern with the first line! Make each line intriguing enough to get the reader to the next. Make this only one sentence. The shorter and more punchy the better. Meat: Where you are teaching something, the information you want to convey, the value that the audience takes away Takeaway OR CTA: The last thing they will remember before you call them to the conversation. Inhibit comments and make it easy for people to add their value or comment on what you left them about. Or, offer them a lesson, a nice takeaway that will leave them with a good impression of you and your business. CTA for copy: Comment with a link to deplatform the audience. “I go more in-depth on this topic in my newsletter or on this free thing Check it out here”. Or if you want to learn more about XYZ, link to the landing page for the offer/booking. Don’t do this if you aren’t offering anything in the post (or it’s not tied to anything you directly offer). For example, if you give away a free guide, and the post is about what the guide is about, link the guide in the comment section. You can also comment or write in the caption to “leave a comment” as well. Typically, this method has been much more effective than the former. CTA for video: Consider only doing 1 promotion for every 2-3 posts. At the end, or somewhere in the middle, ask them to comment a word to receive a valuable free thing that relates to the video. Or to learn more about the information you portrayed in the video. Everyone who comments, implicitly, they are interested in your offer. Interact with other creators 30m per day - Highest leverage task if you want to make the most of your content strategy. This is how most businesses can jumpstart virality, think Popeyes and Chick-fil-A chicken sandwich wars. They started with Popeyes commenting on one of Chick-Fil-A’s posts..

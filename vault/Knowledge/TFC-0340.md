# I Have to Get my Lawyer to Review Before Moving Forward

## TL;DR
Media Buying Expert sales training on agency sales, closing, and high-ticket deals.

## Content
8.I Have to Get my Lawyer to Review Before Moving Forward.mp4 Okay, next objection. I have to get my lawyer to review this before I can move forward. I want to be clear. I don't really view this as an objection. I view this as you're just not going to get the deal on that call. You need to schedule a follow-up time when you're actually going to move forward. So we'd want to reiterate to the person when we're going to get that follow-up conversation scheduled. So, as an example, just to role play it, hey, I have to get my lawyer to review this before I can move forward. Sounds fair to me. Interestingly enough, a lot of people who do that, they'll get back to us within a couple days, and usually they'll pull the trigger. Other people, I don't know what kind of lawyers they have, but they take months. Want to reiterate to you, if you do the deal in this time frame, we give you these incentives. If it takes longer than that, then those incentives are stripped away and the deal just, you know, it's not as sweet as it could be if you move fast. Do you think it's fair if we schedule a follow-up time later this week to talk with one another if your lawyer is going to review it in that time frame? Or is your lawyer one of those slow ones that's going to move like a turtle? Hehehehe.

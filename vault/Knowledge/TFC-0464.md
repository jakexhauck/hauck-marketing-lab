If each one of these salespeople and the business owner Are reviewing each other's footage collectively. There is collective advancement that happens. It's a good lesson. It's a fool's game to learn the hard way. It is a wise man's game to learn hard lessons through others. There's no reason that each one of your salespeople or the business owner should learn less. Lessons on their own. They should be learning lessons through a collective effort of all of the organization's calls shared amongst one another.

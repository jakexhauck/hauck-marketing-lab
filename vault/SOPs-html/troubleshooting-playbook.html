Troubleshooting Playbook

Category: Optimization Source: Module 4 Lesson 6 When to use: When something breaks. Match the symptom to the section below. Estimated time: Varies by issue, 10 to 60 min

Prerequisites

A. Ad Account Disabled

B. Policy Violation

C. Campaign Stuck in Learning Phase

D. Sudden CPM Spike

E. Lead Form Not Working (monthly QA)

Notes

  • Bookmark facebook.com/accountquality and Events Manager.
  • Test lead forms monthly for every client. Silent forms kill trust faster than any other failure.
  • Almost all CPM spikes are creative problems. Default response: add more diverse creatives.
  • Significant edits (budget +20%, new creative, audience changes) reset Learning. Batch optimizations.

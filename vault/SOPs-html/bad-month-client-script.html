Present a Bad Month to a Client

Category: Comms Source: Module 4 Lesson 2 When to use: When monthly performance is materially worse than the prior month. Address before client asks. Estimated time: 10 min prep, on the monthly call

Prerequisites

Checklist

Notes

  • Clients don't expect perfection. They expect honesty and a plan. That script keeps them.
  • Full script template: "Hey [client], I want to be upfront. This month wasn't as strong as last month. CPL went from $12 to $18. Here's what happened: our top 3 creatives hit fatigue around week 3 and I didn't refresh them fast enough. That's on me. Here's what I'm doing: I've built 10 new ad variations and am testing customer testimonial videos. Based on the first 48 hours, next month should bounce back. I'll have an update for you by next Friday."
  • Always volunteer this BEFORE the client asks. Proactive ownership = retention. Reactive defense = churn.
  • If you ran into the bad month from a structural problem (offer issue, lead quality issue, fulfillment issue on their side), name it diplomatically and propose a fix.

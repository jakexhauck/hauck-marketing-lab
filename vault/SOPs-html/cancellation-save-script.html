Save a Cancelling Client

Category: Comms Source: Module 3 Lesson 13 When to use: Client says "I want to cancel" or signals churn. Respond same day, not next week. Estimated time: 20 min call

Prerequisites

Checklist

Notes

  • Most cancellations come from feeling unheard, not from bad performance. The diagnostic question alone saves a lot.
  • Full script template: "I hear you. Before we do anything, can I ask, what specifically isn't working for you? [Listen, don't interrupt.] I appreciate you telling me that. Here's what I'd like to do: give me 2 more weeks. I'll [specific action: new creatives, new angles, adjust budget]. If you don't see improvement by [date], I completely understand and we'll part ways. Fair?"
  • Don't discount to save them. Discounting attracts low-commitment clients. Better to let them go than train them that complaining gets price cuts.
  • If the cancellation reason is something structural on their side (their close rate, their fulfillment, their offer), name it diplomatically. You're not on the hook for their sales process.

Notes

  • The price ladder for reference:
  • New clients always enter at the current new price. Never discount to match legacy rates.
  • Most existing clients pick Option A then move to new rate within 3 months anyway.
  • If a client leaves over a price raise, they weren't an ideal fit. Higher pricing filters for better clients.
  • $3K clients are easier than $1.5K clients. Less price-sensitive, more results-focused.

Category: Research Source: Module 5 Lesson 11, Bonus Invisible Skeleton When to use: Day 2 of every onboarding, then re-run monthly per client. Estimated time: 30 to 45 min for first run, 15 min for monthly check

Checklist

Notes

  • Ads running 3+ months are likely profitable. Unprofitable ads stop. Study long-running ads most closely.
  • Check competitors in 2 to 3 other cities to find fresh angles before local saturation.
  • National brands in your niche invest heavily in testing. Adapt their 6+ month ads to local context.
  • Track competitor ad longevity in a simple spreadsheet: competitor, launch date, status. Over months this reveals which styles have staying power.
  • Never copy competitor copy. The goal is patterns and gaps, not plagiarism.

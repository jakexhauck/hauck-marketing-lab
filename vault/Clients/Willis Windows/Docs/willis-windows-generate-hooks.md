# Vortex Brief: Willis Windows Hooks

Sir, the competitor research is unanimous on three things: nobody is matching your $100 offer, every incumbent sounds like a 1995 brochure, and review sections are stacked with no-show and streak complaints. So we attack those three gaps directly. No overlap with what they're already running.

---

## Angle 1: Biggest Offer In Metro Detroit
*Category: Urgency / Scarcity. White space: competitor offers top out at $25 to $50 off. You're at $100 plus free screens. Lead with the math, not manufactured urgency.*

1. **"$100 off your windows. Free screen cleaning. Nobody in Metro Detroit is matching this."**
2. **"Your neighbor paid $25 off. You're getting $100 off plus free screens. Read that again."**
3. **"Spring window slots are filling. $100 off plus free screens, while we still have route space."**
4. **"Metro Detroit's biggest window-cleaning offer right now. Not a gimmick, just the math."**

---

## Angle 2: Young Hustlers, Real Detroit Crews
*Category: Industry Disruption. White space: every incumbent sounds corporate or generic. This voice is yours alone if you take it.*

1. **"The big franchises send uniforms. We send a crew that actually cares if your windows squeak."**
2. **"Forget the corporate window guys. Real Detroit hustlers, on your driveway, doing it right."**
3. **"We're not a 40-year-old franchise. We're young, hungry, and our reviews show it."**
4. **"Tired of window guys who treat your house like job #14? Meet the crew that treats it like #1."**

---

## Angle 3: Same-Week Or It's Free
*Category: Problem Agitation. White space: slow scheduling and no-shows are the #1 complaint in every market we studied. Nobody guarantees against it. You can.*

1. **"Booked a window cleaner who ghosted you? On your driveway within 5 days or it's free."**
2. **"Streaks, no-shows, surprise charges at the door. Here's how we're different, in 30 seconds."**
3. **"Stop chasing window cleaners who don't call back. We show up within the week, guaranteed."**
4. **"Three Detroit window companies cancelled on you? Try the one that puts the guarantee in writing."**

---

## Top 4 To Test First

1. **"$100 off your windows. Free screen cleaning. Nobody in Metro Detroit is matching this."** — Owns ground no competitor can match. Pure pattern interrupt.
2. **"The big franchises send uniforms. We send a crew that actually cares if your windows squeak."** — Plants the young-hustlers flag against Fish and Window Genie. Voice is the moat.
3. **"Booked a window cleaner who ghosted you? On your driveway within 5 days or it's free."** — Punches the #1 review complaint and stakes a guarantee nobody else dares offer.
4. **"Your neighbor paid $25 off. You're getting $100 off plus free screens. Read that again."** — Comparative anchor against the actual market ceiling. Specific, conversational, scroll-stopping.

Sir, the only thing missing before production is ops sign-off on the same-week guarantee. If that lands, Angle 3 becomes your single strongest creative line.

# Audience Research with Nexus

**Category:** Research
**Source:** Module 3 Lesson 10
**When to use:** Day 2 of onboarding, before writing any copy. Research feeds creative messaging, not targeting settings.
**Estimated time:** 30 to 45 min

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] Client business name, product or service, location, price range collected
- [ ] Nexus prompt template ready
- [ ] Client folder created at `clients/[name]/`

## Checklist

- [ ] **Gather inputs:**
  - [ ] Business name
  - [ ] What they sell (specific product or service, not "marketing")
  - [ ] Location (city, state)
  - [ ] Price range
  - [ ] Primary offer they want pushed
- [ ] **Run Nexus prompt, request:**
  - [ ] Top 10 reasons to buy (emotional, not features)
  - [ ] Top 5 purchase objections
  - [ ] Top 5 trigger moments (what makes someone start shopping)
  - [ ] Audience language and slang (actual words they use)
  - [ ] Competitive alternatives (who they shop against)
  - [ ] Relevant daily routine details (where, when, how they decide)
- [ ] **Save output** to `clients/[name]/audience-research.md`
- [ ] **Translate research into ad angles:**
  - [ ] Each "reason to buy" becomes one of the 12 angles for Aurelius
  - [ ] Each "objection" becomes a PAS or BAB ad addressing it
  - [ ] Each "trigger moment" becomes a hook scenario
  - [ ] Audience language replaces generic AI phrasing in copy

## Notes

- Research is for messaging, not targeting. Andromeda finds the audience if creative is diverse and on-message.
- Reasons to buy are emotional, not features. "Saves my Friday night" not "fast delivery."
- Re-run Nexus quarterly or whenever a client's offer changes meaningfully.
- Validate output against real reviews (Google, Yelp, Facebook). If Nexus says "anxious about cost" and 30 reviews mention pricing, it's a real angle.

# Competitor Research Workflow

**Category:** Research
**Source:** Module 5 Lesson 11, Bonus Invisible Skeleton
**When to use:** Day 2 of every onboarding, then re-run monthly per client.
**Estimated time:** 30 to 45 min for first run, 15 min for monthly check

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] Client niche and city confirmed
- [ ] List of 3 to 5 competitor names from client (ask if you don't have them)
- [ ] Claude Code workspace open

## Checklist

- [ ] **Find competitors in Meta Ad Library:**
  - [ ] Go to facebook.com/ads/library
  - [ ] Set country (usually United States) and category to All ads
  - [ ] Search by competitor name, or by niche + city ("Dentist Tampa")
  - [ ] Filter to Active ads only
- [ ] **Collect 5 to 10 active ads per competitor:**
  - [ ] Prioritize ads running 60+ days (proven winners)
  - [ ] Mix formats: image, video, carousel
  - [ ] Screenshot or copy primary text, headline, description
  - [ ] Note start date, page library size (more ads = bigger spender)
- [ ] **Tag each ad line with one of the 10 jobs:**
  - [ ] Pattern Interrupt
  - [ ] Audience ID
  - [ ] Problem Statement
  - [ ] Agitate
  - [ ] Villain / Old Way
  - [ ] Mechanism / New Way
  - [ ] Proof
  - [ ] Benefit Stack
  - [ ] Offer + Risk Reversal
  - [ ] Urgency + CTA
- [ ] **Feed everything to Claude with analysis prompt, request:**
  - [ ] Per ad: hook (and why it works), offer, target audience, emotional trigger, weaknesses, unexplored angles
  - [ ] Across all ads: top 3 patterns, 3 unaddressed gaps, 5 novel angles for differentiation
- [ ] **Save analysis** to `clients/[client]/competitor-analysis.md`
- [ ] **Pull master skeleton:** across the 5 ads, what jobs show up most and in what order? That's the vertical's master skeleton.
- [ ] **Build strategy around gaps, not copies.** Don't replicate competitor ads. Find what they all miss.
- [ ] **Schedule monthly re-check** for first Monday of each month

## Notes

- Ads running 3+ months are likely profitable. Unprofitable ads stop. Study long-running ads most closely.
- Check competitors in 2 to 3 other cities to find fresh angles before local saturation.
- National brands in your niche invest heavily in testing. Adapt their 6+ month ads to local context.
- Track competitor ad longevity in a simple spreadsheet: competitor, launch date, status. Over months this reveals which styles have staying power.
- Never copy competitor copy. The goal is patterns and gaps, not plagiarism.

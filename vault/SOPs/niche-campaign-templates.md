# Quick-Reference Niche Campaign Templates

**Category:** Build & Launch
**Source:** Module 3 Lesson 12
**When to use:** When starting a new client. Match their niche to the closest template, then customize.
**Estimated time:** 5 min to pick, 30 to 60 min to customize

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] Client niche identified
- [ ] Client offer and CTA confirmed
- [ ] Client geographic service area known

## Checklist

- [ ] **Pick the matching template below.**
- [ ] **Customize for the specific client:**
  - [ ] Replace [City] and [Business] placeholders
  - [ ] Match offer to client's actual offer (don't invent specials)
  - [ ] Confirm budget is realistic for their LTV
- [ ] **Build per campaign-structure-naming SOP using these specs.**

### Restaurant / Cafe / Bar

- [ ] Daily budget: $15 to $30
- [ ] Radius: 5 to 10 miles
- [ ] Target CPL: $3 to $8
- [ ] Objective: Traffic or Leads
- [ ] Top format: food close-up video or carousel of 3 dishes
- [ ] Top angle: time-limited deal or hero shot
- [ ] CTA: Get Directions

### Real Estate

- [ ] Daily budget: $20 to $50
- [ ] Radius: 15 to 25 miles
- [ ] Target CPL: $10 to $25
- [ ] Objective: Leads (lead form, not landing page)
- [ ] Top format: lead form with property image or aerial
- [ ] Top angle: Home Valuation (best magnet for sellers)
- [ ] CTA: Get Quote or Sign Up

### Home Services (plumbing, HVAC, cleaning, landscaping, roofing, painting)

- [ ] Daily budget: $20 to $40
- [ ] Radius: 10 to 20 miles
- [ ] Target CPL: $15 to $35
- [ ] Objective: Leads
- [ ] Top format: before/after carousel
- [ ] Top angle: transformation, licensed + insured trust
- [ ] CTA: Get Quote
- [ ] Lead form fields: name, phone, service, address

### Med Spa / Beauty

- [ ] Daily budget: $25 to $50
- [ ] Radius: 10 to 15 miles
- [ ] Target CPL: $15 to $40
- [ ] Objective: Leads
- [ ] Top format: 15 to 30 second treatment video
- [ ] Top angle: first-time offer (low-barrier intro pricing)
- [ ] CTA: Book Now
- [ ] Lead form fields: name, phone, service

### Gym / Fitness Studio

- [ ] Daily budget: $15 to $35
- [ ] Radius: 5 to 8 miles
- [ ] Target CPL: $8 to $20
- [ ] Objective: Leads
- [ ] Top format: class-in-action video
- [ ] Top angle: free trial class
- [ ] CTA: Sign Up or Book Now
- [ ] Lead form fields: name, phone, preferred class time

## Notes

- Templates are starting points, not copy-paste. Always customize location, copy, creatives, and offer specifics.
- For niches not listed: pick the closest match and adjust radius and CPL target.
- Always run the napkin math first: client LTV x close rate x leads must exceed monthly spend + your fee.

# Run Monthly Strategy Call

**Category:** Optimization
**Source:** Module 4 Lesson 2
**When to use:** First week of every month per active client. The single most important retention call.
**Estimated time:** 30 min call + 30 min prep

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] Monthly report sent 1 day before the call
- [ ] Last month's data pulled and analyzed
- [ ] Next month's draft plan ready (3 specific actions)
- [ ] Zoom or phone link confirmed with client

## Checklist

- [ ] **Pre-call prep (30 min, day before):**
  - [ ] Pull last 30-day data: spend, leads, CPL, revenue, ROAS
  - [ ] Compare month-over-month with % change
  - [ ] Identify top performing ad and explain WHY it worked
  - [ ] Identify what didn't work and what you learned
  - [ ] Draft 3 specific next-month actions
  - [ ] Calculate ROI in client's language ($ in, $ out, including repeat customer value)
  - [ ] Send monthly report to client
- [ ] **Call structure (10/10/10):**
  - [ ] **Minutes 1-10: The Numbers**
    - [ ] Walk through monthly report
    - [ ] Lead with the metric THEY care about (leads, revenue, bookings)
    - [ ] Compare to last month
    - [ ] Keep simple, no jargon
  - [ ] **Minutes 10-20: The Learnings**
    - [ ] Show top performing ad
    - [ ] Explain WHY it worked (audience insight, hook, format)
    - [ ] Mention what didn't work and what it taught you
  - [ ] **Minutes 20-30: Next Month Plan**
    - [ ] Present 3 specific things you'll do
    - [ ] New creative angles, budget changes, new offers to test
    - [ ] Ask: any promotions coming up? Seasonal events? New menu items? New services?
    - [ ] Close with "Any questions or concerns?"
- [ ] **Post-call:**
  - [ ] Log next-month actions in client folder
  - [ ] Schedule next monthly call
  - [ ] If a clear upsell opportunity surfaced, schedule that pitch for the following week (not now)

## Notes

- This call is the single biggest driver of 12-month retention vs 1-month churn.
- The 30 minutes positions you as a partner, not a vendor. Without it, you're the person who sends a number every Monday.
- Always include repeat customer math in ROI. First-time order undervalues your work. 50 new customers x 3 orders/year x $35 = $5,250 changes the conversation.
- Only upsell when results are GOOD. Never upsell during a bad month. Wait for wins to build on.
- If client misses the call, reschedule within the week. Skipping a month is how retention drops.

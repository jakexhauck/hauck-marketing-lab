# Pitch an Automation Upsell

**Category:** Upsell
**Source:** Module 5 Lesson 12
**When to use:** Two moments only, at contract signature OR 30 to 60 days post-launch when results are strong.
**Estimated time:** 15 min conversation

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] At least one trigger condition met:
  - [ ] Ad performance is good (specific leads + CPL numbers in hand)
  - [ ] Client said a trigger phrase (see Notes)
  - [ ] At contract signature, in same conversation as the deal
- [ ] You've picked 1 to 2 automations matching their actual gap (don't sell all five)

## Checklist

- [ ] **Pick the automation that fits the trigger:**
  - [ ] "Front desk not following up fast enough" → Lead Follow-Up Automation ($500 to $800/mo)
  - [ ] "Wish we had more Google reviews" → Review Generation System ($300 to $500/mo)
  - [ ] "Do you know anything about email marketing?" → Email Sequences ($500 to $1,000/mo)
  - [ ] "People keep asking questions on Facebook at midnight" → AI Chatbot ($500 to $1,000/mo)
  - [ ] "Where can I see our results?" → Weekly Reporting Dashboard ($300 to $500/mo)
- [ ] **Open with acknowledgment of strong performance:**
  - [ ] "We're generating [X leads/month] at [$Y CPL]"
- [ ] **Name the gap with a specific %:**
  - [ ] "X% of leads aren't being contacted in the first hour"
- [ ] **Cite the benchmark:**
  - [ ] "Conversion is 21x higher when leads are contacted within 5 minutes vs 30+"
  - [ ] Use industry benchmarks if you don't have client-specific data. Never invent numbers.
- [ ] **Describe the automation in plain language:**
  - [ ] "Texts every new lead within 60 seconds"
  - [ ] "Confirmation emails, automatic 24h and 72h follow-ups"
  - [ ] "Books straight to your calendar"
  - [ ] "Zero burden on your team"
- [ ] **Reference a comparable client result:**
  - [ ] "Similar clients see 20 to 40% more bookings"
- [ ] **State the additional cost and ask about timing:**
  - [ ] "It's an extra $[X]/mo. Want me to set it up this week or wait until next month?"
- [ ] **Don't be pushy. A no is fine. Offer again at 30-60 days with real data if rejected.**

## Notes

- Two optimal moments only. Never pitch on the initial discovery call. Trust is earned first.
- Three-tier packaging math: Basic $1,500 (ads only) → Growth $2,500 (+lead follow-up + emails) → Premium $3,500 to $4,000 (+chatbot + reviews + dashboard). Tool costs are $50 to $100/month, everything else is profit.
- Listen for trigger phrases:
  - [ ] "We're getting leads but my front desk isn't following up fast enough"
  - [ ] "I wish we had more Google reviews"
  - [ ] "Do you know anything about email marketing?"
  - [ ] "People keep asking questions on our Facebook page at midnight"
- Tools to use: GoHighLevel ($97 to $297/mo, all-in-one), Make.com (visual automation, cheaper at scale), Zapier (simpler, more expensive at scale).
- Setup is 2 to 4 hours per client. After that automations run themselves. Weekly check-in to verify nothing broke.
- Never set and forget. Broken follow-up = lost leads = damaged reputation.

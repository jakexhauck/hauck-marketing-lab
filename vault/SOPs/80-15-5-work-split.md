# Apply the 80/15/5 Work Split

**Category:** Operations
**Source:** Module 4 Lesson 5
**When to use:** Monthly audit of where your time goes. The principle that scales a freelancer into a business owner.
**Estimated time:** 30 min audit, ongoing rebalancing

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] List of every recurring task you do across all clients
- [ ] Honest assessment of who currently does each task

## Checklist

- [ ] **Map every recurring task to one of three buckets:**
  - [ ] **80% AI handles:** ad copy, creative concepts, audience research, report drafts, optimization suggestions
  - [ ] **15% VA handles:** uploads, formatting, screenshots, scheduling, file org, data entry
  - [ ] **5% YOU handle:** strategy decisions, client calls, approvals, QC, new business
- [ ] **List every task you currently do:**
  - [ ] Screenshot collection from Ads Manager
  - [ ] Report formatting
  - [ ] Creative uploads
  - [ ] Scheduling calls
  - [ ] Drive folder organization
  - [ ] Competitor research saves
  - [ ] Email follow-ups from templates
  - [ ] Ad copy generation
  - [ ] Creative concepts
  - [ ] Audience research
  - [ ] Report drafts
  - [ ] Optimization suggestions
  - [ ] Client calls
  - [ ] Campaign strategy
  - [ ] Kill/scale decisions
  - [ ] Budget changes
  - [ ] Sales calls
  - [ ] Monthly strategy calls
  - [ ] (Add your own)
- [ ] **For each task, ask:**
  - [ ] Does this require judgment? If no, it's not in your 5%.
  - [ ] Can it be taught with a Loom? Then delegate to VA.
  - [ ] Can AI do it 80% of the way? Then it's AI's job and you review.
- [ ] **Reassign anything misaligned:**
  - [ ] If you do something a VA could do, build a SOP and delegate
  - [ ] If you do something AI could draft, build a prompt and review only
- [ ] **Re-audit monthly:**
  - [ ] If anything in your 5% has more than 3 hours/day on it, systems are incomplete
  - [ ] Goal: 2 to 3 hours/day total across 7+ clients

## Notes

- Your 5% is the highest-value work. Decisions, client communication, quality control. Everything else runs without you.
- Never delegate: client communication, campaign strategy decisions, optimization decisions (kill/scale), budget changes, sales calls, monthly strategy calls, ad copy writing (you + AI do this together).
- If a task requires judgment, keep it. If it's repetitive and Loom-teachable, delegate it.
- Your VA handles the hands. You keep the brain.
- Math that makes it obvious: 5 clients x $1,500 = $7,500/mo stuck at 5-6 hours/day. Add a VA at $1,000/mo, you handle 7 clients at 2-3 hours/day = $10,500/mo. Net: $9,500 and growing.

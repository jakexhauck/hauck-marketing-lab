# Send Weekly Client Report

**Category:** Optimization
**Source:** Module 3 Lesson 11, Module 3 Lesson 13
**When to use:** Every Monday for every active client. Same day, same time.
**Estimated time:** 15 to 20 min per client (5 min with VA pre-fill)

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] Daily tracking sheet up to date for the last 7 days
- [ ] Weekly report template ready
- [ ] Client's preferred channel known (text, email, Slack, WhatsApp)

## Checklist

- [ ] **Pull 7-day data from Ads Manager:**
  - [ ] Total spend
  - [ ] Total leads (or purchases for ecom)
  - [ ] Cost per lead
  - [ ] CTR, CPM (for internal context, not always shared)
  - [ ] Best performing ad and its CPL
- [ ] **Fill the template (5 to 8 sentences):**
  - [ ] This week's numbers (spend, leads, CPL, best ad)
  - [ ] What's working (top ad and why it's winning, any trends)
  - [ ] What we changed (paused X underperformers, increased budget on Y, added Z new creatives)
  - [ ] Next week's plan (1 to 2 specific actions)
  - [ ] Close with "Questions? Let me know!"
- [ ] **Sanity check:**
  - [ ] No jargon (no CPM, no ROAS, no impressions unless client asks)
  - [ ] Numbers match what's in Ads Manager
  - [ ] Bad news included if there is any (don't hide)
  - [ ] No fabricated stats or invented results
- [ ] **Send via client's preferred channel** at agreed time

## Notes

- Lead with the number THEY care about. Usually leads or revenue, never impressions.
- Clients churn from silence, not from a bad CPL week. Same day, same time, every Monday, no exceptions.
- Bad week format: "CPL spiked from $12 to $18 because creative fatigue hit our top 3 ads around day 4 and I didn't refresh fast enough. That's on me. I've built 10 new variations and am testing a new angle. Expect a bounce-back by Friday."
- If you're asking the client questions, you've already failed. Updates are proactive.
- Keep total length under 200 words. Nobody reads a five-paragraph report.

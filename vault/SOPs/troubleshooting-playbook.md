# Troubleshooting Playbook

**Category:** Optimization
**Source:** Module 4 Lesson 6
**When to use:** When something breaks. Match the symptom to the section below.
**Estimated time:** Varies by issue, 10 to 60 min

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] Confirm what specifically broke (account disabled, policy violation, CPM spike, lead form silent)
- [ ] Have client's Business Manager open

---

### A. Ad Account Disabled

- [ ] Don't panic. Often reversible.
- [ ] Check email from Meta for the reason (suspicious payment, policy violation, account quality)
- [ ] Go to facebook.com/accountquality
- [ ] Locate disabled account, click Request Review
- [ ] Submit professional appeal explaining business and policy compliance
- [ ] Expect response 24 to 48 hours
- [ ] If denied: open live chat via Business Manager, Help, Contact Support
- [ ] Last resort: create new ad account within same Business Manager
- [ ] **Prevention going forward:**
  - [ ] Verify business in Business Manager
  - [ ] Legitimate payment methods only
  - [ ] Avoid dramatic budget swings
  - [ ] Follow advertising policies

---

### B. Policy Violation

- [ ] Identify which violation triggered (check email from Meta)
- [ ] Fix per category:
  - [ ] **Before/after photos (health, fitness, beauty):** show the process not transformation. "See our approach" not "Look at these results."
  - [ ] **Income or earnings claims:** never promise specific dollar amounts. "Grow your business" not "Make $10K/month."
  - [ ] **Misleading claims:** avoid exaggeration. "Best pizza in town" is fine. "Scientifically proven to cure hunger" is not.
  - [ ] **Personal attributes ("Are you overweight?"):** rephrase as "Want to feel more confident?"
  - [ ] **Missing Special Ad Category:** housing, credit, employment, social issues, politics must be tagged or accounts get suspended.
- [ ] Edit the ad copy or creative, save, re-submit for review

---

### C. Campaign Stuck in Learning Phase

- [ ] Check days since launch and total conversions
- [ ] **WAIT IF:**
  - [ ] Less than 7 days since launch
  - [ ] Some conversions appearing
  - [ ] CPL acceptable
  - [ ] No major edits made
  - [ ] Action: do nothing, let data accumulate
- [ ] **RESET IF:**
  - [ ] 10+ days, no progress
  - [ ] Zero or near-zero conversions
  - [ ] CPL 3x+ target
  - [ ] Multiple edits already restarted learning
  - [ ] Action: duplicate the ad set, pause the original, start fresh

---

### D. Sudden CPM Spike

- [ ] Diagnose in order:
  - [ ] **Creative Fatigue?** Check frequency. If 3+, audience has seen ads too many times. Fix: launch new creative.
  - [ ] **Audience Saturation?** Local with narrow radius can exhaust the pool. Fix: gradually expand radius.
  - [ ] **Competition?** More advertisers in the auction = bidding pressure. Fix: build higher-CTR creative to win auctions.
  - [ ] **Seasonality?** Q4 CPMs spike 30 to 50% from holiday volume. Normalizes in January. Fix: set client expectations in advance.
- [ ] Add 20+ new diverse creatives if creative fatigue is the cause (90% of cases)

---

### E. Lead Form Not Working (monthly QA)

- [ ] Click your own ad, verify form opens
- [ ] Submit a test lead with fake data
- [ ] Check Leads Center (Business Manager, Leads Center), confirm test lead appears
- [ ] If connected to CRM, confirm lead transferred through
- [ ] Confirm client gets email notification (Page Settings if not)
- [ ] Test on mobile AND desktop (forms break on one but not the other)
- [ ] Confirm form has 3 to 4 fields max (name, phone, email, optional qualifier). More than 4 kills conversions.

## Notes

- Bookmark facebook.com/accountquality and Events Manager.
- Test lead forms monthly for every client. Silent forms kill trust faster than any other failure.
- Almost all CPM spikes are creative problems. Default response: add more diverse creatives.
- Significant edits (budget +20%, new creative, audience changes) reset Learning. Batch optimizations.

# Hire and Train Your First VA

**Category:** Operations
**Source:** Module 4 Lesson 4
**When to use:** When you hit 4+ clients, 5+ hours/day on repetitive tasks, and $6K+/month revenue.
**Estimated time:** 1 weekend for setup + training

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] At least 4 active clients
- [ ] Spending 5+ hours/day on repetitive non-judgment tasks
- [ ] $6K+/month revenue (can afford $800 to $1,200/mo VA)
- [ ] Loom account (free or paid)

## Checklist

- [ ] **Decide what to delegate (day 1 tasks):**
  - [ ] Screenshot collection from Ads Manager
  - [ ] Report formatting (template fill)
  - [ ] Creative uploads to Ads Manager
  - [ ] Scheduling client calls
  - [ ] Google Drive folder organization
  - [ ] Competitor research saves
  - [ ] Basic email follow-ups from templates
- [ ] **What NOT to delegate:**
  - [ ] Client communication (calls, strategy)
  - [ ] Campaign strategy decisions
  - [ ] Optimization decisions (kill/scale)
  - [ ] Budget changes
  - [ ] Sales calls
  - [ ] Monthly strategy calls
  - [ ] Ad copy writing
- [ ] **Post job:**
  - [ ] OnlineJobs.ph (Philippines, $5 to $8/hr, recommended for long-term)
  - [ ] OR Upwork ($8 to $15/hr, easier to test, project-based)
  - [ ] Title: "Virtual Assistant for Digital Marketing Agency"
  - [ ] Start part-time (20 hrs/week)
  - [ ] Look for VAs with social media or marketing experience
- [ ] **Record Loom training videos:**
  - [ ] One Loom per delegatable task
  - [ ] Screen record + narrate WHAT you're doing and WHY
  - [ ] 5 to 10 min per video
- [ ] **Write matching SOP docs:**
  - [ ] Google Doc with numbered steps + Loom link at top
  - [ ] Format: Step 1: Open Ads Manager. Step 2: Click [client name]. Step 3: Screenshot the dashboard.
- [ ] **Live training session (30 min):**
  - [ ] VA shares screen
  - [ ] They do the task while you watch
  - [ ] Correct mistakes in real-time
- [ ] **First week schedule:**
  - [ ] Day 1: watch all Looms, read all SOPs, take notes, ask questions
  - [ ] Day 2: screenshot all client dashboards (you review), organize one client's Drive folder
  - [ ] Day 3: format one weekly report using template (you review before sending)
  - [ ] Day 4: upload creatives to Ads Manager for one client (you review before publishing)
  - [ ] Day 5: full workflow across ALL clients (you review everything)
- [ ] **Review cadence:**
  - [ ] Daily review for first 2 weeks
  - [ ] Spot-check weekly after that

## Notes

- A VA at $5 to $8/hr can handle 60% of the work that's currently eating your day.
- Test with a small paid task before committing on Upwork.
- The math: $1,000/mo VA cost lets you scale from 5 clients ($7,500/mo, 5-6 hrs/day) to 7 clients ($10,500/mo, 2-3 hrs/day). Net $9,500/mo.
- Don't hire too early. Below 4 clients, you don't have enough volume to justify the cost or the SOPs to delegate effectively.

# Raise Prices on an Existing Client

**Category:** Comms
**Source:** Module 4 Lesson 3
**When to use:** When you've earned it. Never reactive, always strategic.
**Estimated time:** 15 min at the end of a monthly call

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] At least one trigger has been met:
  - [ ] 3+ months of strong consistent results with data supporting value
  - [ ] Referral coming in (they're a fit at higher tier)
  - [ ] You're at capacity (filtering for better-fit clients)
- [ ] Most recent month was a strong one (never raise during a bad month)
- [ ] Your new price point on the ladder is decided

## Checklist

- [ ] **Timing:**
  - [ ] End of a monthly call, in person via Zoom or phone
  - [ ] NEVER via text or email
- [ ] **State the change as already decided:**
  - [ ] "Hey [name], I wanted to bring something up. We've been working together for [X] months now, and the results have been really strong (specific result, e.g. 'we generated 340 leads at $11 each last quarter')."
  - [ ] "I'm adjusting my pricing for new clients to $[new price]/month starting next month."
- [ ] **Acknowledge their tenure:**
  - [ ] "I wanted to give you a heads up because you've been with me from early on."
- [ ] **Offer two options:**
  - [ ] Option A: grandfather current rate for 3 months as a thank you, then move to new rate
  - [ ] Option B: move to new rate now, add a bonus (extra creative refresh, priority support, etc.)
- [ ] **Let them choose:**
  - [ ] "Either way works for me. What feels right for you?"
  - [ ] Don't push. Don't apologize.
- [ ] **If pushback, demonstrate ROI:**
  - [ ] Show the math, $X spent generates $Y revenue
  - [ ] "Would you pay $3,000 to make $20,000? That's what we're doing every month."
  - [ ] Don't defend the price. Reframe the value.

## Notes

- The price ladder for reference:
  - [ ] Starter: $1,500/month (first 1-3 clients, building portfolio)
  - [ ] Established: $2,500 to $3,000/month (after 3-6 months with proven results, case studies, systems)
  - [ ] Premium: $5,000+/month (6-12 months in, managing $10K+ monthly spend, multi-platform)
- New clients always enter at the current new price. Never discount to match legacy rates.
- Most existing clients pick Option A then move to new rate within 3 months anyway.
- If a client leaves over a price raise, they weren't an ideal fit. Higher pricing filters for better clients.
- $3K clients are easier than $1.5K clients. Less price-sensitive, more results-focused.

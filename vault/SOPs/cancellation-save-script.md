# Save a Cancelling Client

**Category:** Comms
**Source:** Module 3 Lesson 13
**When to use:** Client says "I want to cancel" or signals churn. Respond same day, not next week.
**Estimated time:** 20 min call

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] Hop on a call, not a text exchange
- [ ] Recent performance data pulled
- [ ] Mental state: calm and curious, not defensive

## Checklist

- [ ] **Stay calm. No panic, no apologies before listening.**
- [ ] **Ask the diagnostic question:**
  - [ ] "Before we do anything, can I ask, what specifically isn't working for you?"
- [ ] **Listen fully. Don't interrupt.**
  - [ ] Let them finish
  - [ ] Take notes
- [ ] **Acknowledge their concerns:**
  - [ ] "I appreciate you telling me that"
  - [ ] Mirror back what you heard to confirm understanding
- [ ] **Propose a concrete 2-week plan:**
  - [ ] "Here's what I'd like to do: give me 2 more weeks. I'll [specific action]"
  - [ ] Specific action options: 10 new creatives, new angle test, budget adjustment, new offer, switch from broad to retargeting layer
  - [ ] Set the deadline: "If you don't see improvement by [date], I completely understand and we'll part ways"
- [ ] **Empower their decision:**
  - [ ] "Fair?"
  - [ ] Don't beg. Don't discount. Don't promise impossible results.
- [ ] **If they accept:**
  - [ ] Schedule the check-in for the deadline date
  - [ ] Execute the specific action that day
  - [ ] Send daily updates that week (more visibility than usual)
- [ ] **If they still cancel:**
  - [ ] Thank them, ask for a referral or testimonial if appropriate
  - [ ] Document why in client folder (pattern recognition for future clients)
  - [ ] Off-board cleanly: hand off ad account, send final report

## Notes

- Most cancellations come from feeling unheard, not from bad performance. The diagnostic question alone saves a lot.
- Full script template: "I hear you. Before we do anything, can I ask, what specifically isn't working for you? [Listen, don't interrupt.] I appreciate you telling me that. Here's what I'd like to do: give me 2 more weeks. I'll [specific action: new creatives, new angles, adjust budget]. If you don't see improvement by [date], I completely understand and we'll part ways. Fair?"
- Don't discount to save them. Discounting attracts low-commitment clients. Better to let them go than train them that complaining gets price cuts.
- If the cancellation reason is something structural on their side (their close rate, their fulfillment, their offer), name it diplomatically. You're not on the hook for their sales process.

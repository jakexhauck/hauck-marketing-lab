# Vortex Pre-Launch Creative Diversity Check

**Category:** Creative
**Source:** Module 3 Lesson 9, Module 3 Lesson 7
**When to use:** After all creatives are built, before campaign launch. Last creative gate before QA.
**Estimated time:** 5 min

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] All planned creatives generated and exported
- [ ] Client approval received

## Checklist

- [ ] **Count creatives by format:**
  - [ ] Single image: ____
  - [ ] Carousel: ____
  - [ ] Video (15 to 30s): ____
  - [ ] UGC-style (looks phone-shot): ____
  - [ ] Before / After (split-screen): ____
  - [ ] Meme or relatable: ____
- [ ] **Pass criteria (all three must be true):**
  - [ ] At least 15 total unique creatives
  - [ ] At least 4 different formats used
  - [ ] At least 7 different angles (one reason to buy each, see ad-copy SOP)
- [ ] **Check for duplicates and near-duplicates:**
  - [ ] No two ads with the same image and only different headlines
  - [ ] No two ads testing the same angle with the same format
- [ ] **Check both export sizes exist:**
  - [ ] 1080 x 1080 (square, feed)
  - [ ] 1080 x 1920 (vertical, stories and reels)
- [ ] **Decision:**
  - [ ] Pass: proceed to pre-launch QA
  - [ ] Fail: build more creatives until pass criteria met

## Notes

- Creative IS the targeting under Andromeda. Less than 15 creatives starves the algorithm.
- A "meme or relatable" creative is high-leverage because it doesn't look like an ad. At least one per client.
- Format diversity matters more than count. 5 single images is worse than 5 mixed (image, carousel, video, UGC, before/after).
- If 90% of performance problems trace to creative, this gate is the cheapest insurance you have.

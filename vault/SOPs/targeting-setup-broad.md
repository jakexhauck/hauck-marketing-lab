# Set Up Broad Targeting (Andromeda Era)

**Category:** Build & Launch
**Source:** Module 3 Lesson 10
**When to use:** Every new ad set. Replaces all complex interest-based targeting.
**Estimated time:** 5 min per ad set

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] Business type identified (local vs online)
- [ ] Client address or service area confirmed
- [ ] 15 to 25 diverse creatives ready (creative IS the targeting)

## Checklist

- [ ] **Set location:**
  - [ ] Drop pin or enter business address
  - [ ] Toggle "People living in or recently in this location"
  - [ ] Set radius by business type:
    - [ ] Restaurant or cafe: 5 to 10 miles
    - [ ] Dentist or doctor: 10 to 15 miles
    - [ ] Gym or studio: 5 to 8 miles
    - [ ] Real estate: 15 to 25 miles
    - [ ] Home services: 10 to 20 miles
    - [ ] E-commerce or online: entire country
    - [ ] Coaching or virtual service: entire country
- [ ] **Set age range only if relevant:**
  - [ ] Bar or alcohol: 21+
  - [ ] Med spa: typically 25 to 55
  - [ ] Most other niches: leave default (18-65) unless there's a clear reason
- [ ] **Skip everything else:**
  - [ ] No interests
  - [ ] No behaviors
  - [ ] No lookalikes
  - [ ] No detailed targeting expansion
  - [ ] No saved audiences from past campaigns
- [ ] **Add retargeting (Week 2+ only):**
  - [ ] Skip on Day 1 launch
  - [ ] After 2 weeks of traffic, create separate ad set for:
    - [ ] Website visitors (last 30 to 90 days)
    - [ ] Video viewers (watched 50%+)
    - [ ] Customer email list
    - [ ] Page or IG engagers (last 30 days)
    - [ ] Lead form openers who didn't submit

## Notes

- Andromeda evaluates 10,000x more ad candidates per auction. Broad lets it work. Narrow targeting starves it.
- Creative IS the new targeting. A pizza ad showing a family dinner targets families. The same pizza ad about late-night munchies targets college students. Don't pre-filter what the algorithm can find better.
- If a creative fatigue or low CTR problem appears, the answer is always more diverse creatives, not narrower targeting.
- Audience research (Nexus) still matters, but it feeds creative messaging, not Ads Manager settings.

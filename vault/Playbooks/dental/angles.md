10 angles that consistently outperform generic "great smile" creative for dentists.

1. **No-judgment angle.** "Haven't been to the dentist in 5 years? We won't lecture you." Pulls in the avoidance cohort.
2. **Whole-family convenience.** "One office, every kid, every appointment, same Saturday." For parents.
3. **In-network insurance call-out.** Name the top 3 insurers in the market. "We take Delta, Cigna, BCBS, no surprise bills."
4. **Same-day relief.** "Tooth pain right now? We see emergencies today, not next week."
5. **Before/after Invisalign.** Real patient photos. Caption: "8 months. No braces. No one at work noticed until the reveal."
6. **Free whitening hook.** "Your first cleaning includes free professional whitening. Saves you $400."
7. **Trust through tenure.** "Dr. [Name] has been doing this for 22 years in [neighborhood]." Local-first.
8. **Veteran/family-owned.** "Family-owned since [year]. Not a chain. Not a corporate practice."
9. **Cosmetic transformation story.** Customer voiceover, 15 seconds: "I hated my smile for 12 years. Six months and I cannot stop grinning."
10. **Second-opinion angle.** "Got a $5,000 treatment plan from another dentist? Bring it in. Second opinion is free. Most patients leave paying less."

Bonus tactical angles: 24-hour booking guarantee, kid-distraction (TV in every room), payment plans, gentle-dentistry for anxious patients.

Family and cosmetic dental patients split into two cohorts.

Primary: parents 30 to 55, household income $70k+, scheduling cleanings for kids and themselves, choose convenience and trust over price. Hate dental offices that feel cold or push upsells. Want one provider for the whole family, evening and Saturday slots, in-network with their insurance.

Secondary: adults 25 to 60 seeking cosmetic work (whitening, veneers, Invisalign). Higher income, longer consideration window. Trigger events: wedding, new job, post-divorce, photo in the mirror they could not unsee. Will research for weeks. Want clean, modern offices, confident reviews, real before-and-after photos (not stock).

Top objections: cost, fear of pain, time off work, judgment about how long they have neglected their teeth. Address judgment directly. Lead with "no lectures, no guilt."

Language they use: "haven't been in a while," "need a deep clean," "want my smile back," "scared of the dentist," "in-network with [insurance]," "same-day," "no surprise bills."

Daily routine: check email morning and evening, schedule on phone between meetings or after kids are in bed. Most-likely booking windows: 7-9 AM and 8-10 PM.

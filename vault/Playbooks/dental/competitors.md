The dental competitive landscape is crowded but predictable. In any metro you will see:

- 2 to 4 corporate chains (Aspen Dental, Western Dental, Heartland) running the same "free exam + X-ray" hook on Meta. Their creative is glossy stock + clinical white. Easy to differentiate against by leaning into local + family-owned.
- 5 to 10 independent practices running boosted Facebook posts of "Happy Friday from our team!" content. Not real ads. Not in the auction in any serious way.
- 1 to 3 well-run independents that have hired an agency. They will run testimonial UGC + before/after carousels. These are the real competition. Differentiate on offer (free whitening beats $99 special on perceived value) and angle (no-judgment, whole-family convenience).

What everyone runs and looks the same: $99 cleaning specials with generic smile imagery, dental chair stock photos, "schedule today" CTAs without an actual offer.

Lanes to own:
- Free whitening anchor offer (most competitors do discount-only)
- Same-day emergency positioning
- Specific insurance call-outs (most generic-ify)
- Cosmetic specialization (Invisalign-only or veneers-only sub-positioning)
- No-judgment / anxiety-friendly messaging

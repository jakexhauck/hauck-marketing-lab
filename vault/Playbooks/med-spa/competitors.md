The med-spa landscape is fragmented. Three competitor types to plan against in any metro.

Established clinics (10+ years in market):
- Run testimonial UGC and credentialed-injector creative.
- Tend to underspend on Meta and overspend on Instagram organic.
- Their audience is loyal. Hard to poach. Differentiate by going after the cohort they're not actively prospecting (men, brides, preventative-30s).

Franchise / chain med-spas (Ideal Image, LaserAway, Sono Bello):
- High-volume, low-touch, lead-magnet driven. Run national-creative-with-local-targeting at scale.
- Heavy on "first treatment $X" anchor offers.
- Beat them on the credibility lane (named injector, board-certification) and personalization. Their creative feels corporate; yours should feel human.

Pop-up injectors and home-based practitioners:
- Often unlicensed or weakly credentialed. Compete on price.
- The credibility lane wipes the floor with these. Lead with credentials, clinic accreditation, RN-injected messaging.

What everyone runs and looks the same:
- "$10/unit botox" price-led ads
- Generic syringe-and-lip close-ups
- "REVEAL YOUR BEST SELF" headlines
- Stock-photo skincare aesthetic
- Before-and-after with obvious lighting tricks

Lanes to own:
- Named, credentialed injector as the face of every ad
- Subtle/believable results positioning ("we say no when the answer should be no")
- Preventative tox for the 30s cohort (nobody markets to them directly)
- Male treatment positioning (separate ad set, male-led imagery)
- Bridal package + group bookings
- Membership model with transparent math

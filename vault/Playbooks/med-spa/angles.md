10 angles that consistently outperform generic "look younger" creative for med-spas.

1. **Subtle-results before/after.** Real patient, 3-month timeline, minor changes. Caption: "Tox + a little filler. No one at her office noticed. Her husband said she looked rested." Honesty about scale wins.
2. **Credentials lead.** 15-second clip of the injector in scrubs: "I'm Dr. [Name], board-certified [credential], 14 years injecting. Here's how I'd treat you." Builds trust faster than any other angle.
3. **Preventative tox.** For the 30 to 40 cohort: "Starting tox in your 30s is not vanity. It's prevention. Here's why." Educational angle.
4. **No-downtime hook.** "Lunch break treatment. Back to your kids' soccer game by 4." For the time-poor parent.
5. **Bridal countdown.** "6 months out from the dress fitting? Here's the schedule." Calendar-driven, predictable conversion.
6. **Real-patient voiceover.** Patient, in their own kitchen, no script: "I was scared. Now I would recommend [clinic] to my sister." More trusted than any production-value spot.
7. **Membership math.** "Members save $1,200/year and get priority booking. Here's the breakdown." For the LTV cohort.
8. **Anti-overdone positioning.** "We say no when the answer should be no. That's why our patients keep coming back." Punches sideways at strip-mall injector culture.
9. **Body sculpting demo.** Time-lapse-style B-roll of CoolSculpting or similar. Caption: "Same pants. 8 weeks." Visual proof.
10. **Behind-the-scenes credibility.** Walk through the clinic: clean room, real RNs, named instruments. Trust through transparency.

Bonus tactical angles: financing (CareCredit) call-out, group-discount for friends, seasonal "summer skin reset," before-and-after carousels with patient quotes.

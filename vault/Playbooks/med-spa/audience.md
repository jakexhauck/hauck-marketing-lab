Med-spa audience is overwhelmingly female, 30 to 60, household income $80k+, considers cosmetic treatments a wellness expense not a luxury splurge. Two distinct cohorts.

Primary: women 35 to 55, married or partnered, post-kids, returning to the workforce or hitting a milestone (40th, 50th, kid in college). Want results that look like a well-rested version of themselves, never overdone. Will research clinics for weeks. Trust signals matter more than price.

Secondary: brides-to-be and brides' parties, 28 to 45. 6-to-12-month consideration window pre-wedding. Want package pricing for botox + filler + skin treatments. Often book in groups.

Tertiary: men 35 to 55 seeking botox + jaw filler + body sculpting. Quiet cohort, never responds to "ladies night" creative. Needs its own ad set with male-led imagery.

Top objections: looking "done" or frozen, pain, downtime, cost, finding a credible injector. The credibility objection beats every other objection in surveys. Lead with the injector's credentials, training, and years of experience.

Language: "subtle," "natural-looking," "preventative," "tox," "lip filler," "no downtime," "no one will know," "pre-juvenation," "RN-injected," "board-certified," "before-and-after," "consultation."

Daily routine: Instagram heavy, Pinterest secondary. Researches at 9-11 PM on the couch. Books consultations on phone, almost never on desktop. Highest-converting hours: Sunday evening and Monday morning.

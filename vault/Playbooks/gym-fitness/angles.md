10 angles that consistently outperform generic "join now" creative for gyms.

1. **Transformation story.** Real member, 30 to 60 second testimonial, before-and-after with timeline. Caption: "Sarah, 38, two kids, 6 months." This is the hammer.
2. **No-contract, no judgment.** "Cancel anytime. No salesman pitch. No locker-room stares." Pulls the intimidation cohort.
3. **First-week free.** "7 days. No card. Just show up." Lowest friction CTA in the entire vertical.
4. **Class variety.** Carousel of class types. Caption: "Yoga Monday. Strength Tuesday. Boxing Wednesday. Boredom never." For variety-seekers.
5. **Community over chrome.** Group photo, real members laughing post-class. "You don't show up for the dumbbells. You show up for these people."
6. **Coach credentials.** 15-second clip: coach explains why they program a specific movement. Trust + expertise.
7. **Comeback angle.** "Hadn't lifted in 6 years. Walked in nervous. Walked out signed up." For the post-injury / post-baby cohort.
8. **Resolution reframe.** Around Jan 1: "Resolutions die in February. Habits don't. Start one this week."
9. **Wedding/event countdown.** "Wedding in 90 days? Here is the program." Time-pressure trigger.
10. **Specific result metric.** "Average member loses 12 lbs in their first 8 weeks." Numbers beat adjectives.

Bonus tactical angles: founder story for boutique studios, "why we built this" mini-doc, member-of-the-month spotlight, mom-and-pop family-run angle.

Visual style for gym and fitness creatives.

Real-gym energy. Phone-shot, not studio-shot. Sweat is okay. Mid-rep, mid-movement, slightly out of focus is fine and signals authenticity. Light should match the actual facility: natural daylight from a window, warm-tone gym lighting, never the cold sterile glow of a stock photo studio.

Talent: real members or coaches, not models. Body diversity is mandatory; one demographic in every shot kills conversions outside that demographic. Show beginners, not just shredded regulars.

Color palette anchors: bold accent color from the brand on a desaturated background. Grunge texture works for crossfit-adjacent brands; clean minimalist works for yoga/pilates. Logo bottom-right, never on the chest of clothing.

On-camera narration: founder, head coach, or member testimonial. No voiceover by an unrelated narrator. Captions on every video (most viewing happens muted).

Do-nots:
- No stock-photo "fit couple high-fiving on a treadmill"
- No flexing-in-mirror shots
- No "TRANSFORM YOUR LIFE" all-caps headlines
- No fake before-and-after with obvious lighting tricks
- No comparison-to-other-gyms shots, especially with logos visible
- No only-young, only-white, or only-shredded casts
- No bro-science claims ("burn 1000 calories per class")
- No fake urgency ("ONLY 3 SPOTS LEFT") unless verifiable
